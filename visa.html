How it works

Three messages and a courier.

  1. You send us a photo of your passport and your arrival stamp
    On WhatsApp. We tell you the same day whether you qualify and exactly what it will cost, including government fees.
  2. An accredited agency files it
    Vaya never touches the Bureau. A Bureau-accredited agency does the filing, which is the only legal way this works.
  3. Your passport comes back by courier
    We tell you the day it is lodged and the day it is ready. If anything goes wrong you hear it from us first.

Before you ask

The honest part.

  • There is no digital nomad visa. Executive Order 86 created one in April 2025 and it has still not opened. Anyone selling you one is selling you something else.
  • We are not lawyers or immigration agents. We collect your documents and coordinate a Bureau-accredited agency. That agency files. If your case is complicated, we will tell you and send you to someone who can actually advise you.
  • Past 59 days you get an ACR I-Card. That is the thing that unlocks a Philippine bank account and full GCash, so it is worth knowing before you plan your money.
  • We will tell you when you do not need us. If you are leaving in a week, do not pay us.
