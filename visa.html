From the film · EP02 The Visa Truth

You can file it yourself online. Most people miss the date instead.

The Bureau of Immigration runs a portal at e-services.immigration.gov.ph. A straight tourist extension goes through it from your phone, you pay with GCash or a card, and Vaya has nothing to do with it. What the portal does not do is tell you that day 31 is a fine, that day 59 changes what you are, or that the annual report window shuts on 1 March. We track your dates and handle the filings the portal will not take.

How it works

We tell you to do it yourself when you should.

  1. You send a photo of your passport and your arrival stamp
    By email, using the button above. Same day, you get your real dates written down: when your stay ends, when the 59-day line hits, when your annual report is due. That part is free and we do not ask you for anything after it. Once we are working together we move to WhatsApp, because it is faster.
  2. We tell you whether the portal covers you
    If you need a plain 9A extension and your record is clean, the answer is yes, and we send you the link instead of an invoice. Most first extensions are this.
  3. If it is not online, an accredited agency files it
    Vaya never touches the Bureau. A Bureau-accredited agency does the filing, which is the only legal way this works. We quote you the full number first.
  4. Your passport comes back and your dates stay on our calendar
    We tell you the day it is lodged and the day it is ready. Then we tell you before the next deadline, not after.

Before you ask

The honest part.

  • We are not saving you a day at the Bureau, because for most people there is no longer a day to save. We used to say that. The portal made it untrue and we are not going to keep saying it. What we sell is someone holding your dates and dealing with the parts that are still physical.
  • There is no digital nomad visa. Executive Order 86 created one in April 2025 and it has still not opened. Anyone selling you one is selling you something else.
  • We are not lawyers or immigration agents. We collect your documents and coordinate a Bureau-accredited agency. That agency files. If your case is complicated, we will tell you and send you to someone who can actually advise you.
  • Past 59 days you get an ACR I-Card. That is the thing that unlocks a Philippine bank account and full GCash, so it is worth knowing before you plan your money. It is also the part you cannot do from your phone, because they take your fingerprints.
  • We will tell you when you do not need us. If you are leaving in a week, do not pay us. If your extension is a clean 9A and your record is fine, use the portal and keep the PHP 2,500.
