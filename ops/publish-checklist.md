# The publish gate

**Run this on every film before it goes live. Every line, every time.**

**Why it is a written gate and not a habit:** `standards.html` makes twelve promises a stranger can check against any specific film. **A promise with no checklist behind it is a promise that gets kept when there is time.** This is the list that makes the page true.

**Jordan is the last read.** The correspondent and videographer sign their own sections; Jordan signs the whole thing. **Nobody publishes alone.**

---

## 1. Is it true

| # | Check | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every number on screen has its source and the date it was checked**, written down somewhere a stranger could audit | Correspondent |
| 2 | **Every price on screen shows its date on screen** | Videographer |
| 3 | **Estimates are labelled as estimates, out loud** | Correspondent |
| 4 | **Every rent, safety or traffic figure names the district, not the city** | Correspondent |
| 5 | **No health, medical or therapeutic claim survived the edit**, even if someone said it on camera | Both |
| 6 | **Anything AI-generated that appears is labelled where it appears.** No AI footage, voices or people | Videographer |

## 2. Are the people protected

| # | Check | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Signed release for every recognisable face**, counted against the faces in the cut | Correspondent |
| 8 | **Every blur and name-withheld request from a release is honoured in the cut** | Videographer |
| 9 | **Each person has seen their own appearance.** Their clip, not the edit, not the running order | Correspondent |
| 10 | **Anyone whose pay is mentioned agreed to that**, and is paid at or above local market rate through a licensed employer | Jordan |
| 11 | **Sensitive story: second read done by whoever was not behind the camera.** Health, family and money always count | Named before the shoot, not after |
| 12 | **A minor or vulnerable adult: parent or guardian signed**, and the story got its second read | Correspondent |

## 3. Is the money declared

| # | Check | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | **Every named business Vaya earns from carries the disclosure line, spoken on camera, in the same breath as the recommendation.** Not the description, not the end | Both |
| 14 | **Anything comped is said on screen**, and the review reads the same either way | Correspondent |
| 15 | **Nothing in this film was paid for by anyone appearing in it** | Jordan |
| 16 | **Nobody outside Vaya has seen the cut.** Not partners, not the businesses in it, not anyone who pays Vaya anything | Jordan |

**Checks 9 and 16 are the pair that look contradictory and are not.** A person sees **their own appearance**. Nobody sees the piece. If someone has asked for the edit, the answer is no, and `ops/day-one.md` explains why that reading is the one Vaya uses.

**If a line cannot be ticked, the film does not publish.** Not "publish and fix it." The one asset Vaya has is that it is accurate, and it is the only one that cannot be rebuilt.
