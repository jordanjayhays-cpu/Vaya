# Episode brief template

**One page per film. The correspondent fills it, Jordan signs off the question and the code, and nothing gets a shoot date without it.**

`PLAYBOOK.md` section 7 has listed this as missing since September. The four films in `briefs/` are the full shooting briefs; **this is the one-pager that comes first and decides whether a film is worth one.**

**Fill every bracket. A brief with `[BRACKET]` still in it is not a brief.**

---

```
EPISODE BRIEF

Code:            EP__
Working title:   [TITLE]
Brief by:        [NAME]          Date: [DATE]
Signed off:      [JORDAN]        Date: [DATE]

1. THE QUESTION, one sentence, as a viewer would ask it
   [Not a topic. A question. "Is Makati safe" is a question.
    "Safety in Makati" is a topic and topics do not make films.]

2. THE NUMBER
   [One figure that is the spine of the film.
    Source: [WHERE]   Checked: [DATE]   District: [WHICH]
    If there is no number, say so here and explain what carries the film instead.]

3. THE PERSON
   [One named human who lives this. Not a vox pop.
    Release signed: [Y/N]   Blur or withhold requested: [WHAT]]

4. WHAT IT SELLS
   [One of: nothing / Vaya Desk / Vaya Concierge / Vaya Tours / Vaya Home.
    "Nothing" is a valid and sometimes correct answer. EP01 sells nothing.]

5. THE PAGE IT POINTS AT
   [vayanews.com/______    Live: [Y/N]    Header code matches: [Y/N]]

6. THE DISCLOSURE
   [Every business that will be named, and Vaya's relationship to each.
    Which of the three standards lines gets said, and by whom, on camera.]

7. THE ONE THING THAT COULD MAKE THIS FILM WRONG
   [The claim that would embarrass Vaya if it turned out to be false.
    This is the line Jordan reads first.]

8. PRACTICAL
   Shoot days: [N]        Locations: [WHERE]
   Permissions needed: [MALL / BUILDING / GOVERNMENT OFFICE]
   Sensitive: [Y/N]   If Y, second reader: [NAME, not the person who shot it]
```

## The four reasons to reject a brief

**Jordan's job at this stage is to say no cheaply, before anyone spends a shoot day.**

1. **It is a topic, not a question.** Nobody searches for a topic
2. **The number has no source or no date.** `standards.html` promises both publicly, so a film that cannot carry them cannot be made
3. **There is no named person.** Vaya's whole claim is first-hand. A film with no human in it is a blog post with footage
4. **Box 7 is blank.** Every film has a claim that could be wrong. **A correspondent who cannot name it has not thought about it yet**
