**Notes on the invoice, read once**

- **Separate the Vaya fee from everything else, always.** A customer who can see what is ours and what is passed through at cost will trust the next invoice. One blended number looks like a markup even when it is not.
- **Never invoice a percentage of anything.** Not a sale, not a lease, not a placement value. Flat fee for work delivered. This is the single rule that keeps Vaya clear of RESA and broker-dealer law at once.
- **The trading name is Vaya. The legal name on the invoice is [ENTITY LEGAL NAME].** Both appear, so nobody is confused about who they paid.
- **Keep every invoice.** Tax rules outlive your memory of the job.
