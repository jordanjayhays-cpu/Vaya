# Partner agreement

**Not a contract. The email that turns a handshake into a supply chain.**

One of these per partner: the guide, the visa agency, the transport company, the broker. **A WhatsApp "sure boss" is not a supply chain, and you will find that out on the day something goes wrong.**

---

## The email

Subject: **Confirming how we will work together**

Hi [name],

Putting down what we agreed so neither of us is guessing later.

**What you do.** [Exactly what they deliver. Be specific: "run a four hour food tour for up to six guests in Makati, including all food and transport between stops."]

**What we do.** Bring you the customer, handle the booking and the money, and be there. [Add anything else: "film the tour", "collect the documents".]

**The rate.** You invoice us PHP [AMOUNT] per [tour / filing / pickup / month]. We pay within [7] days of the service. **Our price to the customer is our business and your rate does not change based on it.**

**Who employs whom.** Your people are yours. **Nobody working for you is a Vaya employee or contractor**, and you are responsible for their wages, their statutory contributions and their conduct.

**Insurance and liability.** You hold [public liability / professional indemnity / motor] insurance for your side of the work. **We hold our own.** If something goes wrong on your side you handle it, and we will tell the customer honestly what happened.

**Licences.** You confirm you hold [the PRC licence / BI accreditation / DOT accreditation / franchise] required for this work, and you will tell us immediately if that changes.

**Being named.** We publish what we see. **Your name appears on what we make**, and so does your licence number where the law requires it. If you would rather not be named, say so now, because we cannot run it quietly.

**No commission either way.** We do not take a cut of your fee and you do not pay us for referrals. Flat rates in both directions.

**Ending it.** Either of us can stop with [14] days notice. Anything already booked gets honoured.

Reply "agreed" and we are set.

Thank you,
Jordan
Vaya
vayanews.com

## Per-partner notes

| Partner | The clause that matters most |
|---|---|
| **Guide** | Insurance and the licence. A guest injury on an uninsured tour reaches your entity |
| **Visa agency** | BI accreditation, and **confirmation they file annual reports too** |
| **Transport** | Motor insurance, and that drivers are their employees, not yours |
| **Broker** | **PRC licence number, and that they take their commission from the landlord and nothing from us.** Plus: are they willing to be filmed |
