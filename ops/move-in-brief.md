# Move In: the intake call

**Twenty minutes on a video call. PHP 45,000 rides on getting this right, and the questions people do not expect are the ones that find the right flat.**

**Take notes in the call. Send a written summary within an hour and ask them to correct it.** Everything after this is built on what you wrote down.

## The basics

1. When do you land, and when do you need to be in?
2. How long are you staying? **(Under three months, stop. Tell them so and point them at a serviced apartment for free.)**
3. Who is coming? Partner, children, a parent, a pet?
4. What is your total monthly budget for rent, and is that before or after utilities?
5. Is anyone paying part of it? An employer, a relocation allowance?

## How you actually live

6. **Where will you be most days?** An office, home, a client site. This decides the district more than anything else on this list.
7. What time do you start work, and who are you on calls with? **(A US-hours schedule changes which building works.)**
8. Do you cook, or is this a three-deliveries-a-day household?
9. Gym, pool, or neither? **(Building amenities are half the price difference.)**
10. Will you have a car or a driver, or is it Grab and walking?

## The questions that find the real answer

11. **What went wrong in your last place?** The most useful question here.
12. **What would make you move out after three months?** Noise, a commute, a lift, a neighbour.
13. Have you been to Manila before, and where did you stay?
14. **What have people told you about Manila that worries you?** Answer it honestly on the call.
15. Furnished, unfurnished, or do you want us to furnish it?
16. How important is a view, on a scale where one is not at all?
17. Anything non-negotiable? A bathtub, a second bedroom, a specific school run.

## Money and paperwork

18. Do you have your visa sorted, or is that something we should be doing too?
19. **Can you produce proof of income or an employment letter?** Landlords ask, and it delays everything if it is not ready.
20. How are you paying the deposit, and from where? **(Two months deposit plus one month advance is normal. Make sure they know before we start.)**

---

## What you say back, before you end the call

- **What happens next and when.** Five options, filmed, within [X] days.
- **What you will not do.** Not brokers, not negotiating on their behalf, not holding their deposit.
- **That you might tell them to take none of the five**, and that if so you go again once at no extra cost.
- **The fee, and that it is payable now and is not contingent on them signing anything.**

## The red flags

**Any of these and slow down.**

- **They want you to negotiate the price for them.** That is brokerage. Not yours to do.
- **They want to send you the deposit to hold.** Never. It goes to the landlord or the broker, direct.
- **They will not say their budget.** Usually means they do not have one yet, and the search will go in circles.
- **They are moving in under two weeks.** The real timeline is three. Say so or you will fail them.
