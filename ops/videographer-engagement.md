# Videographer engagement letter

**Send before the first shoot day. Get it signed before anyone picks up a camera.**

## Why this exists

**Philippine IP Code section 178.4:** where a work is commissioned by someone who is not the creator's employer and who pays for it, **the commissioner owns the work but the copyright stays with the creator, unless there is a written stipulation to the contrary.**

**So paying a videographer does not give you the copyright in your own films. A signed sentence does.** `DECISIONS.md` already records these engagements as "work for hire, Vaya owns the footage." Under Philippine law that is only true if it is written down.

**Retrofit anyone already booked.**

## The letter

Subject: **Engagement terms, [SHOOT DATES]**

Hi [name],

Confirming what we agreed so we both have it in writing.

**The work.** [NUMBER] films shot on [DATES], in [LOCATIONS], delivered as [raw footage plus a graded edit / raw only] by [DELIVERY DATE].

**The fee.** PHP [AMOUNT] total, [half on the first shoot day and half on delivery / in full on delivery]. Travel and equipment [included / billed at cost with receipts].

**Copyright.** You assign to [ENTITY LEGAL NAME] all copyright and related rights in everything you shoot or edit for us under this engagement, worldwide and for the full term of copyright, including the right to edit, publish, license and sub-license it. **This is an assignment of copyright, not only of ownership of the files.** We will credit you on every film unless you ask us not to.

**Your portfolio.** You may show the finished films in your own portfolio and on your own channels. You may not license the footage to anyone else.

**Raw files.** All raw footage, project files and audio come to us on delivery. Keep a backup for 90 days, then it is ours alone.

**Kit and people.** You bring your own equipment and insure it. Anyone you bring is engaged by you, not by us, and you are responsible for paying them.

**What we will not ask for.** We will not ask you to film anyone who has not signed a release, or to enter anywhere we do not have permission to be.

**Cancellation.** If we cancel inside 72 hours of a shoot day we pay [50%] of that day. If you cancel inside 72 hours you help us find a replacement.

Reply with "agreed" and your full legal name and address and that is enough for me.

Thank you,
Jordan
Vaya
vayanews.com

## Before you send

- **Fill every bracket.**
- **A lawyer should read this once**, at the same hour that covers RESA and RA 11965. It is short enough to review in five minutes.
- **UNSURE** whether a reply saying "agreed" satisfies the written stipulation requirement under s.178.4, or whether a signature is needed. **Ask. If a signature is needed, a photographed signed page is fine.**
