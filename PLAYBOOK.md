# The Vaya playbook: how we operate, so the next city runs on rules

v1, 2026-09-06. This is the operating system. Not the day-to-day list (that is `THIS-WEEK.md`), not the brand (`BRAND.md`), not the research (`research/`). This is how Vaya decides, launches a city, staffs it, measures it, and hands it over. Every future city follows it. When a rule changes, this file changes in the same session, and the change goes in `DECISIONS.md`.

## 1. Operating principles

Ten rules that settle most arguments before they start.

1. **Revenue before video.** A service earns first, then the film about it is made. Films are the funnel, not the product.
2. **Demand pass before slate.** No city gets a series or a page before `research/<city>-demand.md` answers who arrives, who has money, who is growing, and what the constraint is. Manila skipped this and was rebuilt.
3. **Every episode is bookable.** If a film has no paid thing attached, it isn't scheduled.
4. **Own the route, rent the delivery.** Vaya designs the product, sets the standard, sells, and owns the customer thread. Licensed local partners deliver. Vaya never employs drivers, nurses, or guides directly.
5. **Curate, never list.** Ten hosts per city, each with a film. A marketplace is Viator.
6. **Neighborhood, not city.** Every claim, price, and safety number is attached to a named district.
7. **Host-led, bench-backed.** A named local face per series, paid per film, with a second host and second videographer on the rate card so nothing stops when one person does.
8. **The receipt on everything.** Every film, every newsletter, every quote ends on real numbers in the receipt format.
9. **No sponsors.** Money comes from bookings. Partners are paid to deliver, never to be recommended, and always disclosed.
10. **One city until the test passes.** Section 4 defines the test. Until then, the next city is a hypothesis in a research file, nothing more.

## 2. Decision rules

If-then rules. When a question comes up, check here first. If the answer isn't here, it goes to Jordan and then gets written here.

| Question | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Should we add a service?** | Only if all three: a filmed person had the problem, a licensed partner can deliver it within 30 days, and Vaya's take is at least 20% or a flat fee that covers the coordination. |
| **Should we make a series?** | Only if a paid thing already exists for it, and the first episode has a real number for the open. |
| **Should we film this person?** | Only with a signed release before the first frame, a screening call, and, for health, family, or money stories, a second read by someone who isn't the filmmaker. |
| **How do we price a tour or experience?** | Partner's net rate plus 20 to 30%, or a flat guide fee with Vaya selling at retail. Priced in pesos, dollars shown beside. Never below the local market's top tier; Vaya is the easy version, not the cheap version. |
| **How do we price a service (visa, care, driver)?** | Pass-through costs at receipt, plus a Vaya fee. Per-filing or per-placement fee first; a subscription once the customer needs it more than three times a year. |
| **How do we pay a host or videographer?** | Flat fee per film, work for hire, Vaya owns the footage. About US$500 per film in Manila for host plus videographer plus basic edit. Optional PHP 500 per booking traced to the film. No equity, no revenue share. |
| **Who is the employer?** | The licensed partner or the client. Never Vaya. If Vaya is scheduling, paying, and directing a worker, stop and restructure. |
| **Should we take a sponsor?** | No. If a brand wants in, it becomes a partner that delivers something a customer books, disclosed on screen. |
| **Should we build software?** | Not before ten hosts and fifty bookings in a city. Until then: static site, payment links, a newsletter tool, WhatsApp, a spreadsheet. |
| **Should we hire?** | Under five full-time people per city, ever. Ops coordinator part-time at booking ten. Videographer retained at film four. Everyone else is a partner or a freelancer. |
| **Should we say something negative on camera?** | Yes if it's true, sourced, and about a fact, not a person's character. Anything naming a business gets the fact-check step and, until the media lawyer says otherwise, a read for libel risk. |
| **Should we go to the next city?** | Only when section 4's test passes in the current one. |
| **Who decides what?** | Jordan: pricing bands, partners, hiring, city choice, brand. City lead: routes, stories, hosts within the bar, the weekly newsletter. The board records every decision that lands on a person. If it is not on the board, it does not exist. |

## 3. The city launch playbook

Seven stages. Each has an exit gate, an owner, an artifact, and a time budget. A city does not skip a stage. Manila is at stage 3 going on 4.

| Stage | Name | Exit gate | Owner | Artifact | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Demand pass** | The four questions answered with sources: who arrives, who has money and stays, who is growing, what the constraint is. Audience order and lead revenue family named. | Jordan plus research | `research/<city>-demand.md` | 1 week |
| 1 | **Slate and mix** | Seven or fewer series, each with a paid thing, a hook, and a first-money test. Revenue lines ranked by the data. Season one named: four films in order. | Jordan | `research/<city>-series.md` | 1 week |
| 2 | **Supply** | Five hosts signed under the host bar (section 5), one per revenue line, each with a deal in writing and a film date. One licensed partner for each service line. | Jordan sells, city lead sources | Host agreements, partner agreements, the partner kit | 4 weeks |
| 3 | **Page and services** | The city page live from the Manila template. Services listed with starting prices and a contact route that works. Newsletter form capturing. | Claude builds, Jordan approves | `<city>.html`, service cards | 1 week |
| 4 | **Season one** | Four films shot to the kit, four episode codes live, the receipt on each, released on one launch day with newsletter issue one. | City lead and videographer | Films, thumbnails, captions, releases | 6 weeks |
| 5 | **First money** | Ten paid bookings across at least two lines, revenue per film known for every episode, one film that out-earns the rest identified. | Jordan | The numbers sheet | 60 days from launch |
| 6 | **Handover** | The city lead runs a month without Jordan: dates, hosts, newsletter, numbers. Three revenue lines each cover their own cost. | City lead | Monthly city review, the P&L | 90 days from launch |
| 7 | **Next city** | Section 4's test passed. Stage 0 begins for the next city, with the local correspondent hired first. | Jordan | New demand pass | |

Total from stage 0 to handover: about six months per city, if nothing slips.

## 4. The city test

A city is done, and the next one may start, when all five are true for a full month:

1. Three revenue lines each pay for themselves.
2. Revenue per film is known for every episode and the top film's shape has been repeated at least twice.
3. The city lead ran the month without Jordan touching a booking, a host, or a newsletter.
4. Ten hosts are live with films, and no single host is more than 40% of bookings.
5. The playbook was followed without a new exception being written.

If four of five hold, fix the fifth. Do not start the next city on four.

## 5. Roles, per city and central

**Central (Jordan, and Claude as the desk):** brand, the kit, pricing bands, contracts and templates, partner deals, hiring, city choice, the board, the numbers.

**Per city:**

| Role | Does | Paid how | Bench |
|---|---|---|---|
| **City lead** (the correspondent) | Faces the series, sources hosts within the bar, designs routes with Jordan, writes the weekly newsletter, runs the WhatsApp thread until an ops coordinator exists | Flat fee per film plus a monthly retainer once the city passes stage 5 | A second host who has filmed at least once |
| **Hosts** (guides, nurses, drivers, brokers) | Deliver the product under the Vaya name | Net rate, flat fee, or referral fee, through their licensed employer | Two per line minimum |
| **Videographer** | Shoots to the kit, two angles, b-roll list, delivers the package | Per film, retained at film four | A second on the rate card |
| **Ops coordinator** (part-time, from booking ten) | Dates, guests, hosts, reviews, the thread | Monthly | |

**The host bar, five checks, every city:** lives in the city; already delivers the thing; will appear on camera; agrees to Vaya's route or standard; passes a reference call. Licensed where the law requires it (guides, nurses, drivers, visa filing).

**What we take from each host:** a film about them, a page, and bookings they didn't have to find. That is the pitch, in every city.

## 6. Cadence

| Rhythm | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | Newsletter goes out with one episode and one paid thing. Bookings counted by episode code. Every open comment answered. | City lead, Jordan reviews |
| **Twice a month** | One film released, to the kit, with its receipt and code. | City lead, videographer |
| **Monthly** | City review: revenue per film ranked, conversion, view duration, hosts signed, bookings per week. One decision written to `DECISIONS.md`. | Jordan with the city lead |
| **Quarterly** | Slate review against the numbers. Kill the series that earns least, double the one that earns most. Re-run the price scaffolds. | Jordan |
| **Per city, monthly** | A P&L: bookings, pass-through costs, Vaya margin, host fees, film costs. Each city carries its own. | Jordan |

## 8. The numbers, per city

Three that matter, in order: revenue per film, conversion (views to emails to bookings), average view duration. Supporting: hosts signed, bookings per week, share of bookings by host, cost per film.

Measured by episode code, in a sheet, until the volume needs a tool. Judged monthly. A film is judged on what it earned in 30 days, never on views.

## 9. What is fixed and what is local

**Fixed across every city:** the name form (Vaya plus city), the brand, the kit, the receipt, the five voice rules, the host bar, the pricing method, the no-sponsor rule, the film signature, the launch stages, the city test.

**Local to each city:** the audience order, the revenue mix, the series subjects, the routes, the hosts, the partners, the prices themselves, the newsletter's second edition if the audiences split.

The mistake to avoid: copying Manila's slate into Cebu. Copy the playbook. Rerun the demand pass. Let the data set the slate.
