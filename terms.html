What we are

We coordinate. Licensed people deliver.

For most of what we sell, we are not the one doing the regulated part. An accredited immigration agency files your visa. A licensed broker handles your lease. A licensed transport company drives you. A licensed guide runs the tour. A licensed agency employs any staff we place.

That means: we are responsible for coordinating the service, being honest with you, and getting our part right. We are not responsible for the professional judgement of a licensed provider, and we will always tell you who is actually doing the work.

  • We are not lawyers and nothing we say is legal advice.
  • We are not immigration agents or consultants. We collect documents and coordinate an accredited agency.
  • We are not real estate brokers. We do not transact, negotiate on your behalf, hold deposits, or take commission.
  • We are not a medical or clinical provider. Where care staff are placed, a licensed agency employs them and a physician directs care.
  • We are not a tax adviser, accountant, or financial adviser.
Booking and paying

You pay before we start.

  • Our fee is quoted before you commit and is payable in full up front, unless we have agreed a staged schedule in writing.
  • Third party costs are separate and at cost. Government fees, agency fees, tolls, deposits, rent, courier. They appear on your receipt with what they were for.
  • We never take a commission, a success fee, or a share of any transaction. Our fee is flat, and it does not change based on what you decide.
  • Prices are in Philippine pesos. Any dollar figure shown is a conversion on the day and is not a fixed price.
  • A booking is confirmed when we say so in writing, not when you pay.

  • Inspections are observations, not surveys. We are not engineers, surveyors, or licensed inspectors.
  • We cannot exclude liability for death, personal injury caused by our negligence, or fraud. Nor would we want to.
