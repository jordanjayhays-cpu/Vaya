About

The product is local, all the way down.

Every Vaya city is a small local newsroom: a journalist and a videographer who live there, paid to show you the real thing — the costs, the neighborhoods, the work life, the culture, the opportunities. No parachuting in. No postcards. When Vaya grows, a local creative economy grows with it. That's the point.

Vaya means “go” in Spanish. It also spells what we do: Voices Around Your Area. Say it before a city — Vaya Manila — and it's an invitation. Go find out.
