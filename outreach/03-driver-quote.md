# Quote request: car with driver, 22-day month

Send to three transport companies. MTSC Transport Services publishes a chauffeur-driven service and a landline on its site (verify there). The Klook Manila car-with-driver supplier is a second. Ask a Placewell contact for a third.

---

Subject: Quote for a sedan with driver, 22 days, 10 hours a day, Makati and BGC

Hello,

I'm Jordan Hays from Vaya Manila, a services company for foreigners staying in Manila for a month or more.

We're looking for a regular supplier of a sedan with an English-speaking driver for our clients.

Could you quote the following?

A sedan with driver, 22 days in a month, 10 hours a day, based in Makati and BGC, the same driver for the month where possible.

Your overtime rate per hour, and your out-of-town rate for a day trip to Tagaytay.

A 4-hour block rate for airport pickups and short bookings.

Confirmation of comprehensive and passenger accident insurance on the vehicle.

We expect two to four clients a month to start.

Thank you,
Jordan Hays
Vaya, Voices Around Your Area
vayanews.com

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong, and the fix

1. **Quotes come back on different bases.** Ask every company for the same four numbers so they compare. Put them in one table before choosing.
2. **Insurance is assumed, not confirmed.** Ask for the policy name and cover in writing. No document, no supplier.
3. **The driver changes every day.** "Same driver for the month" is the product. Make it a condition, not a preference, and ask what happens on his day off.
