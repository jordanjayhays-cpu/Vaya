# Facebook group post: finding the retiree with a nurse

For expat retiree groups in the Philippines. Read the group rules first; many ban promotion, and this is not promotion, it is a casting call with nothing for sale.

---

Looking for one person to film, no product, no pitch.

I'm making a short honest video series about what it's actually like to retire in Manila.

The first episode is about care: a retiree here who has a full-time nurse or live-in caregiver, and what that costs compared with home.

If that's you, or someone you know, I'd like to spend one ordinary day with you both on camera, with the nurse's pay and your costs shown honestly.

You keep control: you see the cut before it goes out and can pull out at any point before then.

Message me or comment and I'll send the details.

Jordan, Vaya Manila

---

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong, and the fix

1. **The group reads it as an ad.** No link, no brand pitch, no "our service." The last line is a name, not a URL.
2. **The nurse's side is missing.** Say on the call that the nurse is a subject too, is paid for her time on film, and sees the cut.
3. **Someone volunteers who shouldn't be filmed.** A vulnerable person, a family in conflict, a care situation that's not fine. The pre-call is a screening call. If it feels wrong, it's not the story.
