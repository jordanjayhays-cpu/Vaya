# To Venus, via Airbnb messaging

Send from Jordan's Airbnb account. Airbnb messages are short and cannot carry links to outside booking, so this asks for a call, not a deal.

---

Hi Venus,

I'm Jordan, I'm building Vaya Manila, a small video newsroom about living in Manila.

Your Makati street food tour is the best reviewed food experience in the city and I've watched your videos.

We're designing a Makati to BGC food route and a curated night out, and we want a guide who lives it to run them under the Vaya name.

You'd be paid a flat fee per tour or a net rate, whichever you prefer, and we'd film a short piece about you and your tour for our channel at no cost.

Could we talk for twenty minutes this week or next?

Thank you,
Jordan
Vaya, Voices Around Your Area
vayanews.com

---

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong, and the fix

1. **Airbnb flags it as off-platform solicitation.** Keep the first message about a call. Do not mention prices, links, or "book with us" until you are on a call.
2. **She has an exclusivity clause.** Ask on the call, before anything else. If she does, the ask becomes a co-branded route on her own listing, not white-label.
3. **She reads it as competition.** The line about paying her and filming her is the reason it isn't. Lead with that on the call.
