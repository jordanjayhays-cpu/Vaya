# Outreach 10: the IE Philippine clubs

**There is no public email address for either club.** Both live on IE's community platform, which you log into as a student.

- **IE Alumni Philippines Club**: ieconnects.ie.edu/ieaphilippines/home
- **IE Philippine Club** (current students): ieconnects.ie.edu/iephilippine/home

**Join both first, then post or message.** The club page will show officers and a contact route once you are in. If neither offers one, IE Alumni Relations is at ie.edu/alumni/contact.

**This is an introduction, not an ask.** You want to be a known name before you need anything, which is why nothing in it requests a favour.

## The message

**Subject, if it needs one: American IMBA student moving to Manila in January**

Hi everyone,

I am Jordan Hays, currently on the International MBA here in Madrid, and I am moving to Manila in January 2027.

Not for a posting, and not for a year out. I am starting a company there and staying.

A small video newsroom about what Manila is actually like for people arriving from abroad, with a few practical services alongside it: visa extensions filed through an accredited agency, arrival support, food walks with a licensed guide, and helping people find and move into a home through a licensed broker.

The films are free and always will be. We only charge for things we actually do.

I am joining this club fifteen months early on purpose, because I would rather learn what I am getting wrong now than discover it in February.

Three things I would genuinely value, whenever anyone has a moment.

What foreigners consistently get wrong about doing business in the Philippines, especially the parts that sound fine in a deck and fail on the ground.

Whether Makati or BGC is the better base for someone whose work is filming the city rather than sitting in an office.

And anyone I should be talking to. Not introductions, just names I should know about.

If any of you are in Manila and would let me buy a coffee when I land, I would like that. And if anyone here is heading the other way, from the Philippines to Spain, I have been in Madrid a while and am happy to return the favour.

Thank you,
Jordan
vayanews.com

## Notes before posting

- **Join first, read the feed, then post.** A first post that arrives before you have read anything reads like a broadcast
- **Post to the alumni club and the student club separately.** Different rooms, and the student club is people you can meet on campus this month
- **Do not link the four product pages.** One link to the site is enough. This is an introduction, not a pitch
- **Say you are American.** It is relevant context in a Philippines club, it will come up anyway, and volunteering it is better than having it noticed

## Top three ways this goes wrong

1. **It reads as a soft sell for a company.** That is why the ask is advice and the offer is a favour in return. If you find yourself adding a product list, delete the whole paragraph
2. **Nobody replies.** Normal and fine. The point is that your name is now in the club before you land. Go to an event, post something useful about someone else's question, and try again in a month
3. **Somebody offers to introduce you to a broker or an agency, and you take it before the lawyer hour.** The RESA and recruitment questions are still open. **Accept the introduction, say you are not ready to sign anything yet, and mean it**
