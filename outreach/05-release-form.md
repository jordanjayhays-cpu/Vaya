# Vaya appearance release

Draft for Jordan's approval. Not legal advice. Have a Philippine lawyer check it before the first shoot, especially the data privacy line, since the Philippines has a Data Privacy Act.

---

**Vaya Appearance Release**

I, ______________________, agree to appear in a video and related material produced by Vaya (Voices Around Your Area).

**What I'm agreeing to**

Vaya may film and record me, and may use my name, likeness, voice, and the things I say in the video, on Vaya's website, YouTube channel, social media, and newsletter, worldwide, with no time limit.

Vaya may edit the material. Vaya will not use it in a way that misrepresents what I said.

**What I keep**

I will be shown the finished video before it is published.

I can withdraw from the video at any time before it is published by telling Vaya in writing. After publication, Vaya will consider reasonable requests to remove or blur my appearance.

I can ask for my face to be blurred or my name withheld, and I have told Vaya my choice here: ______________________.

**Money**

I am not being paid to appear, unless stated here: ______________________. I am not entitled to any share of income from the video.

**My information**

Vaya will keep my contact details only to communicate with me about this video and will not share them.

**Signature**

Name: ______________________
Date: ______________________
Signature: ______________________

For Vaya: ______________________

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong, and the fix

1. **Signed after filming, or never.** Signed before the first frame, photographed, and stored. No release, no footage used.
2. **A worker signs under pressure.** For a nurse, a driver, or any staff member in a story, they sign separately from their employer or client, and the "paid to appear" line is filled in.
3. **A minor or a vulnerable adult appears.** Parent or guardian signs, and the story gets the second read described in `research/story-briefs.md`.
