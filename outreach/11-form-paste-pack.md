# Paste pack: the targets with no email address

Written 2026-10-10. **These are finished messages for organisations that publish a contact form or a phone number and no address.** Nothing here is in Gmail, because there is nowhere to send it from. Open the form, paste the body, send. Each one is short on purpose, because form fields are small and nobody reads a wall of text in one.

**Order them by what they unblock.** 01 and 02 are worth doing this week. The rest can wait for a quiet hour.

---

## 01 · The Savvy Expat, the competitor who already sells all four products

**thesavvyexpat.com/form.** Book the free call. **This is the single most valuable hour available to you right now**, and it costs nothing.

Evan sells rental finding, visa help, SIM and utilities, banking and lifestyle setup, claims 200+ expats relocated, six years in country. That is Vaya's entire launch offer, already running, with a head start.

> I am moving to Manila in January 2027 and I will need most of what you do: somewhere to live, the visa dates handled, a SIM and a bank account that actually works.
>
> I should also say plainly that I am starting something in the same space. A small video newsroom about Manila called Vaya, with a services side attached. So I am a genuine customer and also a future competitor, and I would rather you knew that before the call than after it.
>
> If you would rather not take the call on that basis I completely understand.

**Why the disclosure.** You are going to publish films about Manila relocation. If he finds out afterwards that you booked a sales call under cover, that is the story. Say it first and the worst case is he declines.

**What to listen for, not ask:** what he charges, what people buy first, what people ask for that he will not do, and whether anyone asks him in a language other than English.
