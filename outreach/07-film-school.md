# Outreach 07: film school OJT coordinators, for the videographer

**Send to:** the internship or OJT coordinator, not to students. Schools circulate paid opportunities to a whole cohort, they vet, and going through the school keeps the arrangement legitimate.

- **De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde**, BA Film. Malate, closest to Makati and BGC
- **UP Film Institute**, Diliman. The strongest programme. Quezon City, so an hour each way in traffic
- **Asia Pacific Film Institute**. Smaller, industry-focused

**Find the actual coordinator's name and current programme title on the school's site before sending.** A letter addressed to "To whom it may concern" gets filed.

**Send in November or early December**, so it lands before the second-semester OJT placement cycle rather than during it.

---

## The email

Subject: **Paid freelance camera work for a film student, two days a month, starting January**

Hello [name],

I am Jordan Hays. In January I am moving to Manila to start Vaya, a small video newsroom about what the city is actually like for people moving here.

I am looking for one student or recent graduate to shoot with me, and I would rather go through you than post it somewhere.

Here is the whole thing.

Two shoot days a month, in Makati and BGC, plus the edit. Paid per month for delivered work, not per hour. The rate is [PHP amount] a month, and I will pay it whether or not I like what I get, because that is how a rate works.

Their name is on every film we publish. They keep the footage for their reel.

It is not a runner job. They are the camera.

Three things I want to be straight about.

This is freelance, not employment. They keep other clients, they use their own gear, and they invoice me. If that does not fit how your programme handles OJT credit, tell me and I will structure it however your school needs.

I am one person, not a production company. Some months are one shoot day, not two, and I will pay the same either way.

I am new to Manila and I will get things wrong. They will be shooting a foreigner learning the city, which is the actual subject of the films.

If you would circulate this, I would ask for two minutes of footage they shot and cut themselves. Not a group project showreel. Two minutes of their own work tells me everything.

Happy to talk on a call, or to send this in whatever format your office needs.

Thank you,
Jordan Hays
Vaya, Voices Around Your Area
vayanews.com

---

## Before sending

- **Fill in the rate.** PHP 12,000 to 15,000 a month covers two shoot days plus two finished cuts. Never send this with a bracket in it
- **Ask about equipment in the first reply.** Most schools do not let their gear leave campus for commercial work. Either the student owns a camera or Vaya rents one, and you need to know which before January
- **Do not call it an internship.** An internship implies a training relationship and a lower rate. This is freelance work at a real rate

## Top three ways this goes wrong

1. **The school reads it as unpaid or underpaid student labour.** It is not, and the email says the rate and says you pay whether or not you like the work. If they still object, ask what their programme requires and meet it
2. **You get the enthusiastic one instead of the good one.** The two-minute rule is the whole filter. Enthusiasm tells you nothing. Watch the footage before the call
3. **They vanish during exams or thesis season.** Expected. Ask in the first call when their heavy weeks are and plan the shoot calendar around them, rather than being surprised in March
