# Outreach 08: booking one hour with a Philippine lawyer

**Send to:** two or three Philippine law firms or solo practitioners who handle foreign-owned businesses. Look for corporate and commercial practice with foreign investment experience.

**This is one paid hour, not a favour.** Say so. You get better lawyers and faster answers when you open with the fact that you are paying.

**This gates four products.** Visa, Tours, Home and Care all wait on these answers.

---

## The email

Subject: **One paid consultation hour, foreign-owned media and services company, Manila**

Hello,

I am Jordan Hays, an American moving to Manila in January 2027 to start a small company.

I would like to book one paid consultation hour, and I will send my questions in advance so the hour is spent on answers rather than background.

The company is Vaya. Two things under one name.

A video channel about living in Manila, published free, with no advertising sold.

A services business: visa extension filings through an accredited agency, airport meet-and-greet, food tours through a licensed guide, and helping people find and move into a home through a licensed broker.

My five questions.

**1. Mass media ownership.** The Constitution reserves mass media to Filipino citizens. I am American and I publish video about Manila with no advertising revenue. Where is the line, and does a free YouTube channel with no ad sales fall inside or outside it? This is the question I care most about.

**2. RESA and real estate.** I want to charge a client a flat fee to find them a home and get them settled, with a PRC-licensed broker sourcing the options and handling the lease. I take no commission and no share of the transaction. Does the flat fee put me inside the definition of real estate service practice?

**3. Tours.** What is required for a foreigner to sell a paid walking tour where a licensed Filipino guide delivers it? Who needs the accreditation, and what does the arrangement between us need to look like?

**4. Contractor or employee.** I want to pay a videographer per month for delivered work, two shoot days, their own equipment, other clients. What keeps that a genuine independent contractor relationship rather than employment with back pay attached?

**5. Ownership and capital.** A services company with a foreign owner. What are my realistic options on the Foreign Investments Act thresholds, and what would you recommend for a business this small?

I would also like you to look at one page of wording I use with people I film, to confirm the copyright assignment and the Data Privacy Act consent are adequate.

What is your hourly rate, and what is your availability in the next few weeks? I am in Spain, so a video call is easiest.

Thank you,
Jordan Hays
Vaya, Voices Around Your Area
vayanews.com

---

## Before sending

- **Send the questions in advance, as written.** A lawyer who has read them gives you an hour of answers instead of an hour of orientation
- **Ask two or three firms, not one.** Compare the rate and, more usefully, compare how they answer question 1. The one who engages with it seriously is the one to hire
- **Take notes and write them into `DECISIONS.md` the same day.** An answer you cannot find in six months was not worth paying for

## Top three ways this goes wrong

1. **You get a general answer to question 1 and accept it.** Mass media ownership is the question that decides whether Vaya works at all in its current shape. Push for a specific answer about a free, unmonetised channel, and get it in writing
2. **The lawyer sells you an incorporation instead of an hour.** Say clearly you want advice first and a structure decision second. If they will only do the package, that is a signal
3. **You wait until January.** All five questions are answerable now, from Spain, over a video call. Every month you wait is a month of building on assumptions
