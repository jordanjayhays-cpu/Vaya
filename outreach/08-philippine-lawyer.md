# Outreach 08: booking one hour with a Philippine lawyer

**Send to:** two or three Philippine law firms or solo practitioners who handle foreign-owned businesses. Look for corporate and commercial practice with foreign investment experience.

**This is one paid hour, not a favour.** Say so. You get better lawyers and faster answers when you open with the fact that you are paying.

**This gates four products.** Visa, Tours, Home and Care all wait on these answers.

---

## The email

Subject: **One paid consultation hour, foreign-owned media and services company, Manila**

Hello,

I am Jordan Hays, an American moving to Manila in January 2027 to start a small company.

I would like to book one paid consultation hour, and I will send my questions in advance so the hour is spent on answers rather than background.

The company is Vaya. Two things under one name.

A video channel about living in Manila, published free, with no advertising sold.

A services business: visa extension filings through an accredited agency, airport meet-and-greet, food tours through a licensed guide, and helping people find and move into a home through a licensed broker.

**Six questions, reordered 2026-10-07.**

**1. Immigration, and the one I need soonest.** For a holder of an ACR I-Card, does filing the annual report through the Bureau's eServices portal satisfy the requirement in full, or is personal appearance still required? The portal accepts the filing and Bureau notices have said appearance is mandatory, and I cannot reconcile the two. **`visa.html` now publicly says we do not know and promises to publish the answer there**, so this is a live commitment, not curiosity.

**2. Mass media ownership.** The Constitution reserves mass media to Filipino citizens. I am American and I publish video about Manila with no advertising revenue. Where is the line, and does a free YouTube channel with no ad sales fall inside or outside it? This is the question I care most about.

**3. RESA and real estate.** I want to charge a client a flat fee to find them a home and get them settled, with a PRC-licensed broker sourcing the options and handling the lease. I take no commission and no share of the transaction. Does the flat fee put me inside the definition of real estate service practice?

**4. Tours.** What is required for a foreigner to sell a paid walking tour where a licensed Filipino guide delivers it? Who needs the accreditation, and what does the arrangement between us need to look like? **Related, and it decides whether a lapsed guide is disqualified: is DOT accreditation legally required to run a paid tour, or is it a market formality?**

**5. Contractor or employee.** I want to pay a videographer per month for delivered work, two shoot days, their own equipment, other clients. What keeps that a genuine independent contractor relationship rather than employment with back pay attached?

**6. Ownership and capital.** A services company with a foreign owner. What are my realistic options on the Foreign Investments Act thresholds, and what would you recommend for a business this small?

I would also like you to look at one page of wording I use with people I film, to confirm the copyright assignment and the Data Privacy Act consent are adequate.

What is your hourly rate, and what is your availability in the next few weeks? I am in Spain, so a video call is easiest.

Thank you,
Jordan Hays
vayanews.com

## Before sending

- **Send the questions in advance, as written.** A lawyer who has read them gives you an hour of answers instead of an hour of orientation
- **Ask two or three firms, not one.** Compare the rate and, more usefully, compare how they answer question 2. The one who engages with it seriously is the one to hire
- **Take notes and write them into `DECISIONS.md` the same day.** An answer you cannot find in six months was not worth paying for

## Top three ways this goes wrong

0. **You send this to an address that does not exist.** `executive@romualdezlaw.com` came from a search result, not from the firm's own site. **If it bounces, use the forms at carpolaw.com, asglawpartners.com and abolawfirm.ph and paste the body in.** A bounce costs nothing here, but check it landed.
1. **You get a general answer to question 2 and accept it.** Mass media ownership is the question that decides whether Vaya works at all in its current shape. Push for a specific answer about a free, unmonetised channel, and get it in writing
2. **The lawyer sells you an incorporation instead of an hour.** Say clearly you want advice first and a structure decision second. If they will only do the package, that is a signal
3. **You wait until January.** All six questions are answerable now, from Spain, over a video call. Every month you wait is a month of building on assumptions
