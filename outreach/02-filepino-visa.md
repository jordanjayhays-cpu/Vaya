## Top 3 ways it goes wrong, and the fix

1. **They quote retail, not partner, pricing.** Say "fixed per-case fee for a recurring flow" and give them a number of cases you can commit to. Even five a month changes the conversation.
2. **They want to own the customer.** Agree in writing that Vaya is the client of record and the customer's contact stays with Vaya. They file, you manage.
3. **Passport handling isn't defined.** Ask how they receive and return passports, whether they use a tracked courier, and who signs. Write that into the agreement.
