# The foundations

Date: 2026-09-19. **Not marketing. The things that have to exist for Vaya to be a business rather than a project.**

The test for every item: **what breaks the day someone first pays you?**

---

## Tier 1: before a single peso changes hands

If these are missing, you cannot take money. Not "should not". Cannot.

| # | What | Why it breaks without it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Confirm the invoicing entity** | Someone pays you. Whose name is on the invoice? The decision is the existing family business entity, but **"Kinsol" is an unregistered placeholder.** You need its actual registered name, confirmation it can issue an invoice for these services, and who signs | Decided in principle. **Not confirmed** |
| 2 | **Wise account, separate Vaya balance** | There is nowhere for the money to land, and the playbook requires a per-city P&L. **Commingled with agency money and you can never tell whether Manila works** | **Not done** |
| 3 | **A payment link** | A customer says yes and you have no way to charge them. Wise link is enough to start | **Not done** |
| 4 | **Terms, privacy and refunds on the site** | You are selling services to consumers with no stated terms. The first dispute has no reference point, and a refund argument becomes a personal argument | **Not done** |

**These four are the whole of Tier 1 and none of them takes more than an hour.**

## Tier 2: before the first film and the first tour

| # | What | Why it breaks without it |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Videographer copyright assignment, in writing** | **Under IP Code s.178.4 the creator keeps the copyright unless there is a written stipulation.** Pay a videographer on a handshake and Vaya does not own its own films. One clause, before the first shoot day |
| 6 | **General liability insurance** | A guest is injured on a food tour. Without cover it reaches the family entity, and the whole point of using that entity was speed, not exposure |
| 7 | **Media liability insurance** | The first film names a business and gets it wrong |
| 8 | **Appearance releases in use** | Someone on camera withdraws after publication. `outreach/05-release-form.md` exists; have the lawyer check it |
| 9 | **The lawyer hour** | Four questions, one sitting: **RESA** (can Vaya charge a flat fee for Move In), **RA 11965** (can Vaya sell a care placement), **the mass media restriction** (Vaya is a foreign-owned company that publishes), and **the standards page** |

## Tier 3: before it is a company rather than a hustle

| # | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **One spreadsheet. Episode code against every peso** | Revenue per film is the only success metric that counts. Without it you will be guessing in month three, and you cannot backfill it |
| 11 | **The four partner agreements in writing** | Guide, visa agency, transport, broker. **Not a signed contract each, but an email that states the rate, who employs whom, and who is liable.** A WhatsApp "sure boss" is not a supply chain |
| 12 | **Your tax position** | See below. **Nobody has looked at this and it is the real gap** |

## The gap nobody has looked at: tax

**This has not been raised once in any document in this repo, and it should have been.**

You are a resident of Spain, earning money from services delivered in the Philippines, invoiced through a family entity, from customers who are mostly American.

**Open questions, none of which I can answer:**
- Where is this income taxable, and does Spanish tax residency pull it in?
- Does invoicing through the family entity change where it lands?
- Does delivering services in the Philippines create a taxable presence there?
- Does any of this affect your student status in Spain?
- At what point does the Philippine side need its own registration?

> **This is not a "sort it later" item.** Getting it wrong for a year and fixing it retroactively is expensive and stressful. **One hour with an accountant who handles cross-border, before the first invoice, is the cheapest hour in this document.**

**BOARD:** one hour with a cross-border accountant, before the first customer pays.

## What is NOT a foundation

Being clear about this matters as much as the list above, because these feel urgent and are not.

| Not a foundation | Why |
|---|---|
| A company name or logo | You have one. It is on a live site |
| A newsletter provider | Nice. Not load-bearing |
| A second city | Not until ten paid bookings |
| Films | **The business works with no films at all.** Films are how customers find you, not what makes you a business |
| Any software | The playbook forbids it before ten hosts and fifty bookings |
| A Philippine entity | The decision is to use the family entity until the MVP proves out. That still holds |

---

## The order, and it is short

1. **The four Tier 1 items.** One afternoon. Then you can legally and practically take money
2. **The lawyer hour and the accountant hour.** Two calls. They unblock Move In, Vaya Care, the standards page and the tax position at once
3. **Insurance quotes to bound cover.** Before the first tour date is published, per the existing decision
4. **The videographer clause.** Before the first shoot day
5. **The spreadsheet.** Before the first peso, because you cannot reconstruct it later

**Everything else on your list is marketing, and marketing without these is a way to acquire liabilities.**

## Accept when

You could take a customer's money tomorrow, issue them a valid invoice from a named entity, point them at written terms, deliver the service with insurance behind it, and record it against an episode code.
