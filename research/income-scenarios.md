## Read the conservative case honestly

**US$5,158 for a year of work.** That is the number to plan against, and it is sobering.

**What it means:**
- **Vaya does not replace your income in year one.** The agency stays. This is not optional
- **It does cover its own costs and prove the model**, which is what year one is for
- **Five customers a month across all products** is roughly what the conservative case is. That is not an ambitious number; it is a "did anyone at all turn up" number

**Where the conservative money actually comes from:**
| Source | Share of margin |
|---|---|
| **Company films** | PHP 208,000, **29%** |
| **Unit Checks** | PHP 114,000, 16% |
| **Desk filings** | PHP 120,000, 17% |
| **Care, placement plus management** | PHP 112,000, 16% |
| Everything else combined | 22% |

> **Four company films are 29% of a conservative year. Twelve Unit Checks are another 16%.** The high-margin, low-volume products carry the year. **Tours feel like the business and are 13% of it.**

## What the conservative case assumes going wrong

Because "conservative" is only honest if you say what it is conserving against.

- **You land with no correspondent.** Films slip, output is 8 not 12
- **Two of four partners do not reply.** Care and Property start late
- **The first two tours run half full.** No discounting, per the playbook, so revenue is just lower
- **Some products never launch at all.** Marriage, Pets, Schools, Business are all at zero
- **No OFW Site Visits.** That whole market is untouched in year one

**Note what this means: the conservative case has most of the product list at zero.** That is the point. It is what happens if only the obvious things work.

---

## The three ways to beat it, in order of leverage

1. **Sell one company film a month instead of one a quarter.** Twelve films at PHP 52,000 is PHP 624,000, **more than double the entire conservative net, from one product.**
2. **Get Desk to twelve filings a month.** That pays a coordinator, and the coordinator makes everything else possible by taking work off you.
3. **Charge properly for Unit Checks.** At PHP 10,000 with almost no cost, this is the highest-margin per-hour work in the business. It is also the one that does not scale, so price it high and do fewer.

## What is deliberately not in these numbers

- **No revenue from anything sold as information.** Per the decision that Vaya sells what it does, the Report, the Rent Index and the briefs are all free
- **No sponsorship.** The standards page rules it out
- **No investment income, no equity, no revenue share.** All blocked on legal answers
- **No second city**
- **No Jordan salary.** These are net figures before paying yourself
