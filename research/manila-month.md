# The Vaya Month: making Manila a place a remote worker stays for 30 days

Date: 2026-09-06. Jordan's decision: Manila should be a digital nomad stop, the product is a one-month stay, and the services sold during that month make it work. Personal driver is the headline service. This doc reconciles that with the demand data and prices the driver.

## Reconciling this with the data

The data says Manila has about 2,400 nomads against Bangkok's 22,800, costs the same, and loses on traffic and street safety. It does not say nomads won't come. It says the constraints keep them away. A packaged month with a driver attacks exactly those constraints: the driver removes traffic stress and the night-safety question, the package removes the setup friction, and the neighborhood (Makati or BGC) removes the safety-score problem.

So the position is not "Manila is a nomad city." It is **"Manila, done the easy way, for a month."** The target is not the nomad picking a city from a spreadsheet. It is:

1. Nomads already on the Southeast Asia circuit (Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Bali, Da Nang) who want a month somewhere new and English-speaking.
2. Remote workers with a reason to be in Manila: a Filipino partner, family, a Fil-Am background, a company with a Manila office.
3. Corporate remote employees on a "work from anywhere" month, sold through their employer (Placewell channel).

**The test:** convince ten people to book a Manila Month within 90 days. If ten book, build the product line. If two book, the driver and the concierge stay as services for retirees and corporate movers, and the nomad month is a content angle, not a product.

## The personal driver, priced

### What a driver costs in Manila
- Family or personal driver salary: PHP 18,000 to 21,000 a month ([Jobstreet, family driver](https://ph.jobstreet.com/career-advice/role/family-driver/salary), [Jobstreet, personal driver](https://ph.jobstreet.com/career-advice/role/personal-driver/salary)); Manila average about PHP 19,574 ([Indeed](https://ph.indeed.com/career/driver/salaries/Manila)). Add 13th month, benefits, and overtime: budget PHP 24,000 to 28,000 all-in.
- Self-drive sedan rental: a Toyota Vios from PHP 1,500 a day, down to about PHP 900 a day on a monthly rental ([Book2Wheel](https://www.blog.book2wheel.com/2026/08/06/what-are-the-to-5-cars-for-rent-in-manila/)); so PHP 27,000 to 45,000 a month.
- Car with driver from a transport company: day rates are quoted on request; overtime is PHP 500 to 850 an hour depending on the company ([Holidify](https://www.holidify.com/pages/renting-a-car-in-manila-5636.html), [MTSC](https://www.mtscph.com/our-services/chauffeur-driven/)). Klook lists a Manila car-with-driver product ([Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/6567-manila-car-rental-with-driver/)). Assume PHP 3,000 to 4,500 for a 10-hour sedan day; UNSURE until three quotes are in.
- Fuel, tolls, parking for a Makati and BGC month: UNSURE, assume PHP 8,000 to 12,000.

Do not employ drivers directly. A foreign-owned startup employing Filipino drivers takes on labor obligations, and one accident becomes Vaya's accident. Model A or B keeps that with a licensed company.

### What Vaya sells
- **Full month:** cost PHP 66,000 to 99,000 (Model A), sold at PHP 95,000 to 130,000. Margin 25 to 30%.
- **Half month, afternoons and evenings:** 22 days x 5 hours, sold at PHP 55,000 to 75,000.
- **Blocks:** 10 x 4 hours, sold at PHP 20,000 to 25,000.

The pitch is not "cheaper than Grab." A Grab habit of six rides a day at PHP 250 is about PHP 45,000 a month, before surge and before the 40 minutes waiting in the rain. The driver is sold on the four things Grab can't do: the same person every day who knows where you're going, nights out with no safety question, day trips (Tagaytay, Batangas, the airport at 4am) with no negotiation, and the hours you get back from Asia's worst traffic. That is a productivity product for a remote worker on a Western salary, and a status product for everyone else.

### The driver standard, so it stays a Vaya product
- Professional license, NBI clearance, five years' driving in Metro Manila, conversational English.
- Comprehensive insurance and passenger accident cover on the car, confirmed in writing by the supplier.
- Same driver for the month, a named backup, WhatsApp dispatch, a Vaya onboarding call.
- Overtime and out-of-town rates agreed in advance and printed on the client's page.
- One review after week one, one after the month. Two bad reviews and the driver is off the roster.

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong

1. **An accident or an incident in the car.** Fix: never employ the driver directly, confirm insurance in writing, print the standard, keep a backup driver.
2. **Underpricing the hours.** Manila traffic turns a 10-hour day into 12, and overtime at PHP 500 to 850 an hour eats the margin. Fix: sell defined hours, print overtime rates, build 10% overtime into the price.
3. **Selling the month to the wrong person.** Someone who wanted a beach books Manila, leaves after a week, and reviews it. Fix: the copy says Makati and BGC, says traffic, says what the driver is for. Vaya's voice is the friend who tells the truth. The month is for people with a reason to be here.
