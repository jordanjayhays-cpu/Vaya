# How many people can Vaya actually serve?

Date: 2026-09-14. Sources inline. **The short answer is the opposite of what a market-size question usually produces.**

---

## The conclusion first

**The market is enormous and almost irrelevant. Vaya is capacity-limited, not demand-limited.**

The pools below run to millions. **Vaya's realistic year-one ceiling is somewhere around 400 to 600 paid transactions**, because every one of them is a person doing a thing, and there is one of Jordan.

> **Vaya needs roughly 0.3% of one of these pools to fill its entire first-year capacity.**

That is liberating, not depressing. **The job is not to find demand. It is to not drown in it, and to shift work off Jordan as fast as possible.**

## Part 2: The real ceiling. What one person can physically do.

A services business is bounded by hours, not by market. Here is the honest arithmetic.

| Product | Time per unit | Realistic weekly | **Year one, at 40 working weeks** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Desk filing** (visa, annual report, pets, documents) | 2 to 4 hours, batchable. Several can be lodged in one trip | 10 to 15 | **400 to 600** if it were the only thing |
| **Unit Check** | **Half a day including Manila traffic.** This is the killer | 5 to 6 | **200 to 240** |
| **Site Visit** (OFW, outside the metro) | A full day | 2 to 3 | **80 to 120** |
| **Care placement** | Weeks. Screening, assessment, matching, onboarding | 1 | **30 to 50** |
| **Tours** | One per weekend day, 6 to 10 guests | 1 to 2 tours | **50 to 80 tours, 400 to 700 guests** |
| **Landing and Driver** | **Partner-delivered.** Scales with the transport company, not with Jordan | Unlimited in practice | **Constrained by demand, not capacity** |
| **Company films** | A week each, start to delivered | 0.25 | **10 to 15** |

### The number
**Jordan alone, doing everything, cannot exceed roughly 400 to 600 paid transactions in year one**, and that assumes he does nothing else, which is impossible because he also has to film, sell, and build the business.

**A realistic year one, with Jordan plus one part-time coordinator plus partners: 300 to 500 paid transactions.**

### What that is worth
At a blended average of about PHP 4,000 per transaction, **400 transactions is roughly PHP 1.6M, about US$28,000 in year-one revenue.**

**That is a real business and a modest one.** It is enough to prove the model, pay a coordinator, and fund year two. **It is not enough to live on in Madrid, which is why the agency stays.**

## Part 3: The distinction that actually matters

**Split every product by who delivers it.**

| **Jordan-bound.** Hard ceiling | **Partner-bound.** Scales without him |
|---|---|
| Unit Check, Site Visit | Landing and Driver (transport company) |
| Company films, Fixer | Food Tour and Nights (guide) |
| Vaya Business setup coordination | Care placements (Placewell) |
| Anything requiring his judgement on camera | Desk filings, **once the checklist exists and a coordinator runs it** |

> **The growth strategy is one sentence: move revenue from the left column to the right.**

**Desk is the most important product in the business for exactly this reason.** It looks like Jordan-bound work, but once the document checklist and the accredited partner exist, **a PHP 25,000-a-month coordinator can run it and Jordan never touches it again.** That is the difference between a job and a company.

## Part 5: One finding that weakens a core product

**The Bureau of Immigration now offers visa extensions online through its e-Services portal, with processing in two to three business days** ([Pana](https://pana.ph/blog/philippines-visa-extension-bureau-immigration-guide), [Philippines Embassy Info](https://philippinesembassyinfo.com/visa-extension)).

**That undercuts the original Visa Desk pitch, which was "we save you a day at the Bureau."** If the queue is disappearing, the queue cannot be the product.

**What Vaya Desk is actually worth paying for, now:**
- **Knowing what to file and when.** Most people do not know the annual report exists until they are fined
- **Chasing it when the portal fails**, which it does
- **The documents**, which is where filings actually get rejected
- **The bundle.** One person handling visa, ACR, annual report and pet import as a relationship, not four separate ordeals

> **UNSURE how complete the online service is, which categories it covers and how reliable it is in practice. Test it yourself in the landing weeks. If it genuinely works end to end, reprice Vaya Desk around the bundle and the deadline-watching, not around the queue.**

---

## What this means for the plan

1. **Stop researching demand. There is enough.** Every pool above is a thousand times larger than the capacity.
2. **The first hire is a Desk coordinator**, not a videographer and not a salesperson. It is the only role that immediately multiplies output.
3. **Prefer partner-delivered products** when choosing what to push. Landing and Tours scale; Unit Checks do not.
4. **Ten paying customers is the real milestone**, not ten thousand readers. The playbook already says this.
5. **Design for a 500-transaction year, not a 50,000-transaction year.** WhatsApp and a spreadsheet are correct at this scale, and the playbook's ban on software before ten hosts and fifty bookings is right.

## Accept when

There is a written year-one target per product, each expressed as a number of people rather than a market size, and the plan for the first hire is a Desk coordinator.
