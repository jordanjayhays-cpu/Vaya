# 1. The Savvy Expat: this is Vaya's offer, already running

**thesavvyexpat.com. Evan, American, six years in the Philippines.**

Their headline: **"Your One-Stop Solution for a Seamless Move to the Philippines."**

What the site says they set up for you:

| The Savvy Expat | Vaya's equivalent |
|---|---|
| Finding your rental | **Vaya Home — Move In** |
| Securing your visa | **Vaya Desk — Visa** |
| SIM cards and utilities | **Vaya Concierge — Landing** |
| Banking and finances | (Vaya does this inside Landing) |
| Lifestyle setup: locations, restaurants, gyms, clubs | **Vaya Tours**, loosely |
| 24/7 access to Evan and his network | The thing Vaya calls "someone on WhatsApp" |

**That is the entire Vaya launch offer, in one programme, sold by one American who got there six years earlier.**

Claimed track record: **200+ expats relocated**, 100+ five-star reviews, a YouTube channel.

## This is more serious than California Expat

California Expat sells **information** at US$39 a month. Vaya decided not to compete there, which was a clean decision.

**The Savvy Expat sells the services.** There is no positioning move that makes this not a competitor.

## What Vaya still has that they do not

Read their page carefully and three gaps show:

1. **No price anywhere.** The whole site funnels to "Book A Call". That is a high-touch, high-ticket, sell-on-the-phone model. **Vaya publishes PHP 2,500 and PHP 45,000 on the page.** Different customer, different trust
2. **No published films.** They have a YouTube channel but the site is testimonials and a booking form, not work you can watch
3. **Everything routes through one person.** "Full 24/7 access to Evan." That is a ceiling, and it is why they sell a programme rather than four products

**And one thing they have that Vaya does not: 200 customers.**

## What to actually do

**Book the call.** It is free, there is no obligation, and it is the single fastest way to learn what a competitor charges, how they qualify, what they deliver and where they struggle. One hour on their calendar is worth more than another week of research.

**Be honest on it if asked.** Say you are building something in the space. The worst outcome is they decline, and the likely outcome is a useful conversation between two Americans doing the same hard thing.

**BOARD: book a Savvy Expat call at thesavvyexpat.com/form. Then write up what they charge and how they deliver.**

## What this changes

`research/partners-sweep.md` concluded that Vaya's gap is audience, not suppliers. **That is still true, but incomplete.**

The Savvy Expat shows the four products are not novel. Somebody is already selling them, to 200 people, without publishing anything watchable.

**Which sharpens what Vaya's actual difference has to be: published prices, published work, and published standards.** Not the services. The services are table stakes and someone got there first.
