# Vaya Visa Desk: someone files for you

## How extensions actually work (sourced, verify before publishing)

- Most nationals enter visa-free for 30 days. The first extension adds 29 days (total 59). Government fee about PHP 3,000 at a district office; in practice expect about PHP 5,000 for a one- or two-month extension once express and legal fees are added ([Pana](https://pana.ph/blog/philippines-visa-extension-bureau-immigration-guide), [Philippines Travels Guide](https://philippinestravelsguide.com/blog/how-to-extend-philippines-tourist-visa-step-by-step-2026)).
- Past 59 days an ACR I-Card (Alien Certificate of Registration) is mandatory: US$50 plus a PHP 500 express fee, and the total for that filing runs PHP 10,000 or more ([GuidePH](https://en.guideph.com/philippines-visa-immigration/immigration-compliance/acr-i-card-requirements-fees-color-coding/), [Expat Compass](https://expatcompassph.com/visas)).
- The ACR I-Card needs one in-person biometrics visit (fingerprints and photo) even if the extension is filed online ([Philippines Travels Guide](https://philippinestravelsguide.com/blog/how-to-extend-philippines-tourist-visa-step-by-step-2026)). Everything else can be done by a representative.
- Extensions can continue up to 36 months for visa-free nationals ([philippinesembassyinfo](https://philippinesembassyinfo.com/visa-extension)). At six months or more, an Exit Clearance Certificate (ECC) is needed before leaving. UNSURE on current ECC fee; verify.
- Bureau of Immigration accredited agencies file on a visitor's behalf. Agencies process remotely when the passport is couriered to them, charging about PHP 700 extra per extension, and first-extension packages through agencies run PHP 6,000 to 9,000 all-in ([Teleport](https://useteleport.com/blog/philippines-tourist-visa-extension), [InCorp](https://philippines.incorp.asia/guides/tourist-visa-extension/)).
- One accredited provider with public contact details: FilePino, BGC, Taguig, BI-accredited, (02) 8478-5826, 0917 892 2337, info@filepino.com, per its own site ([FilePino](https://www.filepino.com/tourist-visa-extension-philippines/)). The Makati satellite office at Ayala Circuit is the one people praise for speed ([Teleport](https://useteleport.com/blog/philippines-tourist-visa-extension)).

The important legal line: only BI-accredited agencies and liaison officers may transact at the Bureau on someone's behalf. Vaya does not file. Vaya sells the desk and an accredited partner files.

## The product

**Vaya Visa Desk.** You never think about your dates again.

What the customer gets:
1. A date tracker. Vaya knows your entry date, your current expiry, and files two weeks before each deadline.
2. The filing, done. Passport picked up by courier or handed to the partner, extension filed by the accredited agency, passport back by courier.
3. The ACR I-Card, coordinated. One biometrics appointment booked at the closest office, a driver if they want one, everything else handled.
4. The exit, handled. ECC filed before a long-stay departure so nobody gets stopped at the airport.
5. One WhatsApp thread. Reminders, receipts, a copy of every stamp.

Who it is for: anyone staying past 30 days. Nomads on the Vaya Month, retirees waiting on an SRRV, corporate movers waiting on a work visa, and Jordan.

The subscription is the better product. A six-month stayer files three times and needs an ACR and an ECC. At PHP 1,500 a month that is PHP 9,000 to Vaya for five touchpoints, predictable, and it keeps the customer in the WhatsApp thread where the driver, the tours and the housing get sold.

Margin check: agency packages already sell at PHP 6,000 to 9,000 all-in for a first extension. Vaya's per-filing price lands in the same band with a better front end. Not cheaper, easier.

## The partner model

- One BI-accredited agency as the filing partner. FilePino is the first call (public contact above). Ask a Placewell contact for a second.
- The deal: the agency files at a fixed liaison fee per case, Vaya owns the customer, the intake, the reminders, and the thread. Vaya pays the agency monthly.
- The courier: a single account with one courier company for passport pickup and return, tracked, signature on delivery. Passports never move without tracking.
- The rule in writing: the partner is the one who transacts with the Bureau. Vaya staff never carry passports to the Bureau or represent anyone.

## Top 3 ways it goes wrong

1. **A passport goes missing.** Fix: tracked courier only, signature both ways, a photo of the passport before it moves, and the partner signs for it.
2. **A missed deadline.** Overstaying carries fines and a possible blacklist. Fix: file at two weeks out, never one; the tracker sends the customer and Vaya both a reminder at 21, 14 and 7 days.
3. **Vaya acts like the agency.** An unaccredited person transacting at the Bureau is a legal problem for the person and the customer. Fix: the partner files, always, and the contract says so.
