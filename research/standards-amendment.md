# The standards amendment: exact wording for Jordan to approve

Date: 2026-09-19. **This is the decision that unblocks paid company films, which are 29% of the conservative year. It needs one yes or no, not more research.**

## The problem

`standards.html` currently says, live on the site:

> **"Nobody pays to be recommended.** Our partners are paid to deliver a service. **No business can buy a place in a film**, on a tour route, or on this site."

> **"We take no sponsors."**

**A company paying Vaya to make a film about itself is a business buying a place in a film.** As written, the MVP breaks the promise that makes everything else believable.

## Option A: amend the page. Recommended.

Add a fifth bullet to the "Four things we will not do" list, and change the heading to five.

> **Some films are commissioned, and we say so.**
> A company can pay us to make a film about itself. When that happens we say it on screen in the first fifteen seconds, and **that company is then permanently ineligible for any Vaya recommendation, ranking or guide.** Paying us buys a film. It does not buy an opinion, and it costs them the chance of one.

**Why this works:** it keeps "nobody pays to be recommended" literally true, because paying actively removes you from recommendations. **The firewall is the point, and it is stricter than what most publishers do.**

**Say it to the client up front.** It is also a useful filter for who wants a film for the right reason.

## Option B: drop the product

Do not make paid company films. **Cost: about PHP 208,000 of margin in the conservative year, the single largest line in it.** The standards page stays as written.

## Option C: do it quietly

**Not an option.** The page is already public. Anyone who reads both catches the contradiction, and that is the kind of thing a commenter finds, not you.
