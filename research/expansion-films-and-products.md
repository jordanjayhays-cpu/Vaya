## 1. Dental and medical tourism. The easiest money on this page.

**The numbers.** Dental work in the Philippines runs **50 to 70% cheaper** than in the West, with savings up to 70% on implants, crowns and veneers; medical procedures generally **40 to 80% less** ([Desi Dental](https://desidental.ph/dental-prices-philippines.html), [Serenity](https://serenitydentalclinic.com/dental-tourism-vietnam-vs-philippines-2026/)). **BGC and Makati already have clusters of internationally oriented dental clinics.** The Philippine health and wellness tourism market was **US$3.28B in 2024 and is projected at US$16.3B by 2030, a 29.66% CAGR** ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2025/05/20/3084769/0/en/Philippines-Health-and-Wellness-Tourism-Market-to-Grow-from-USD-3-28-Billion-in-2024-to-USD-16-3-Billion-by-2030-at-29-66-CAGR.html)).

**Why it fits Vaya better than anyone else.** The dental clinics already exist and already market themselves. What nobody provides is **the week around the procedure**: the airport pickup, the driver to and from appointments while your face is swollen, somewhere decent to recover, and a person on WhatsApp when something hurts at 11pm. **That is Landing plus Driver plus Vaya Line, sold as one package to a patient who has already decided to come.**

| Films | Service | Price scaffold |
|---|---|---|
| *"I got my teeth done in Manila. Here is the whole bill."* One patient, every peso, the clinic named, the result on camera weeks later | **The Dental Week.** Pickup, driver to every appointment, recovery accommodation, Vaya Line, clinic coordination. Clinic pays a referral fee or the patient pays Vaya directly, not both | PHP 15,000 to 25,000 for the week, plus the clinic's own bill |
| *"What a check-up, a blood test and a dental cleaning actually cost here"* | Feeds the Starter Kit and the Before You Land call | |

**Difficulty: low.** One clinic partner in BGC or Makati, and the rest is services Vaya is already building.
**Watch:** the FDA claims rule in `company-films-legal.md`. No outcome promises, no "safe," no "as good as home." Show one person's bill and one person's result, dated.

## 2. Pets. Small, painful, and completely uncovered.

**The process.** A **Sanitary and Phytosanitary Import Clearance (SPSIC)** from the Bureau of Animal Industry via the InterCommerce portal, valid only **60 days**. Plus an ISO-compliant microchip, a vet health certificate dated within **30 days** of arrival, vaccination and deworming records, and a photo of the animal. **Up to three animals on one clearance.** Compliant pets from approved countries are generally released the same day after inspection, but a **7 to 10 day quarantine inspection period may apply**, and missing paperwork means quarantine ([BAI](https://www.bai.gov.ph/Stakeholders/PetImport), [USDA APHIS](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/pet-travel/us-to-another-country-export/pet-travel-us-philippines)).

**Why it matters more than its size suggests.** A retiree deciding whether to move will not leave the dog. **This is not a small question to them, it is the question.** And it is a filing service, which is exactly the Visa Desk muscle.

| Film | Service | Price scaffold |
|---|---|---|
| *"Bringing your dog to the Philippines."* One real arrival, filmed from the vet's office at home to the airport pickup here | **Pet Desk.** Same shape as Visa Desk: we collect the documents, file the SPSIC, meet the animal at the airport. Airport pickup is already Landing | PHP 6,000 to 10,000 plus government fees |

**Difficulty: low.** No partner strictly needed; it is a filing plus a pickup. Confirm whether a customs broker or accredited agent is required.

## 3. Dying here. The most important story nobody has made.

**This is the hardest thing on the page and the most valuable.**

What actually happens ([liveinph](https://liveinph.com/what-happens-when-foreigner-dies-philippines), [US Embassy Manila](https://ph.usembassy.gov/death-of-a-u-s-citizen/), [Australian Embassy](https://philippines.embassy.gov.au/mnla/Deaths.html)):

- The death must be reported to the local health officer **within 48 hours**, and an unembalmed body must be buried inside the same 48 hours
- **If the cause is not obvious, an autopsy is mandatory and takes two to three weeks. Nothing moves until it clears.** No burial, no cremation, no flight home
- **Repatriation of remains: PHP 350,000 to 650,000.** Cremation with ashes transferred home: PHP 125,000 to 185,000. Local burial: PHP 75,000 to 95,000
- **The embassy pays nothing.** It locates next of kin, issues a Consular Report of Death Abroad, and that is all. Every cost falls on the family
- **Bank accounts are frozen the moment the bank hears of the death**
- And the line that will stop a viewer dead: **if the person he lived with was a girlfriend rather than a wife, she has no legal right to the money, the remains, or a single peso of the estate**

**Why Vaya should make this.** The whole retiree thesis is that people should move here. **A company that only tells you the good half is a brochure.** This is the film that proves Vaya is not one, and there is genuinely nothing honest and well-made on the subject.

| Film | Service | Price scaffold |
|---|---|---|
| *"What happens if you die here."* Slow, quiet, no music tricks. The numbers on screen. One family who went through it, if one will talk | **The Estate Pack.** A Philippine will drafted by a lawyer, a named local contact, a document folder, and a repatriation-cost estimate the family can actually plan against. Lawyer delivers, Vaya coordinates | PHP 15,000 to 30,000, lawyer's fee included |

**Difficulty: medium.** Needs one Philippine estate lawyer, and it needs to be handled with real care.
**Do not:** sell insurance, or imply Vaya can act as executor. Refer both.

## 4. The fork: the 2.7 million going the other way

Everything Vaya has built points inward, at foreigners arriving. **In 2025 a record 2.7 million Filipinos deployed overseas**, the top destinations being UAE 397,892, Saudi Arabia 386,699, Singapore 221,492, Hong Kong 202,415, Qatar 160,890, Taiwan 134,363, Kuwait 106,394, Japan 60,748, South Korea 38,390, Malaysia 35,052 ([BusinessMirror](https://businessmirror.com.ph/2026/02/04/ofw-deployments-hit-record-2-7-million-in-2025-as-kuwait-and-europe-open-doors/)). In 2026 **more than 40,000 OFWs were left stranded in Manila** by Middle East deployment bans ([Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/04/06/2519004/40000-ofws-stranded-amid-me-deployment-ban)).

**This is Placewell's actual business, and it is a bigger audience than everything else in this document combined.**

| Films | Who it serves |
|---|---|
| *"What the agency does not tell you."* The real cost of deployment, fee by fee | The worker |
| *"Croatia is hiring."* Non-traditional destinations opening up. Croatia went from 389 to 964 OFWs in a year | The worker |
| *"Forty thousand people, stuck."* The deployment ban, told through one family | Everyone |
| *"What your recruiter is legally required to do"* | The worker, and it is Placewell's credibility |

**Be clear-eyed: this is a second company, not a second series.** Different language register, different platforms (Facebook and TikTok, not YouTube search), different audience, and it monetises through Placewell rather than through Vaya's services. **The synergy is real and so is the distraction risk.**

**Recommendation: park it.** Write it down, do not start it until Manila has ten paid bookings. **But it is the most valuable thing on this page in the long run**, because it is the only idea here where Jordan already owns the supply side.

## What to deliberately not make

Being useful means pruning.

| Skip | Why |
|---|---|
| **Dating, romance, "finding a Filipina"** | Enormous search volume and it would poison the brand permanently. There is no version of this Vaya can make and still be the company that films nurses respectfully |
| **Crypto, forex, "passive income" schemes** | Regulated, scammy adjacent, and it destroys the trust the Desk depends on |
| **General travel vlogging** | Boracay and Palawan are saturated by better-funded channels. Vaya's edge is the boring, useful, city-specific thing |
| **Anything needing an app** | The playbook forbids software before ten hosts and fifty bookings |
| **Nightlife beyond the curated Nights tour** | Already decided: no P. Burgos, no go-go bars |
| **Political commentary** | Libel exposure with zero revenue attached |

---

## The recommendation, in order

1. **Dental Week first.** Easiest money, one partner, uses services already being built, and the patient has already decided to come.
2. **Pet Desk second.** Cheap to add, same muscle as Visa Desk, and it removes a genuine blocker for the retiree audience.
3. **"What happens if you die here" third.** No revenue on day one. It is the film that makes everything else believable, and it is the one competitors will not touch.
4. **The OFW side: written down, not started.**

## Accept when

One dental clinic partner is signed, the Pet Desk filing process is documented end to end, and the estate film has one lawyer attached and one family willing to talk.
