# Vaya Series and revenue streams, v2: rebuilt on the demand data

Date: 2026-09-06. Supersedes `vaya-series.md` and the ordering in `manila-revenue-model.md`. Built on `manila-demand.md`, which should have come first. The rule going forward is at the bottom: no city gets a slate before it gets a demand pass.

## What the data changed

v1 was written for a digital nomad. The data says the nomad is the smallest audience in Manila and the visa they'd need does not exist. The people actually arriving are American and Canadian visitors (many of them returning Filipinos), Western retirees the government is now actively courting, and corporate expats. So the slate and the money re-order around them.

| | v1 assumption | What the data says | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core audience | Digital nomad choosing a base | About 2,400 nomads in Manila, ranked 215th on Nomad List, visa never launched | Nomad drops to fourth. Content stays honest about it but no product is built for it. |
| Biggest audience | Movers | US visitors: 818K in eight months of 2026, the top market for the first time. Many are Fil-Am returnees. 11-night stays, US$2,073 spend. | The two-week visitor and the returnee become the volume audience. |
| Richest audience | Investors | Western retirees: SRRV from US$1,500, couples budgeting US$2,000 to 4,000 a month for decades, age floor now 40, PRA chasing Europeans, Canadians and Americans with no English explainer | Retire in Manila moves from "later" to season one. |
| Steady audience | Not identified | 153,651 registered foreigners, up 13%; BPO and multinationals; Chinese and Indian residents growing fastest | Corporate relocation and the investor line get a real customer base. |
| The conversion problem | Not identified | "Manila" scores 35 on safety, Makati scores 62. Traffic worst in Asia. | Every piece of content is neighborhood-named. Block by Block becomes the flagship. |
| Housing | A referral line | 25% condo vacancy, 79,000 unsold units, Airbnb revenue down 19.5% | A buyer's market. Brokers and developers pay for access to buyers. Housing moves from a fee line to a sponsor line. |
| The gateway | Not identified | Nearly every foreign arrival lands at NAIA, most leave for the islands | A "48 hours in Manila" product captures traffic that skips the city. |
| Growth markets | Not identified | China up 64%, India up 43%, Canada up 16% in 1H 2026 | Canada is reachable in English now. India and China are season two. |

## Every city gets its own P&L, set by its own demand pass

The Manila mistake was designing the slate from the founding idea (nomads) instead of the arrivals data. The fix is a rule: a city gets a demand pass before it gets a slate, and the pass answers four questions.

1. **Who is arriving, by volume?** Source markets, length of stay, spend, the share that is diaspora.
2. **Who has money and stays?** Retiree visas, registered residents, corporate footprint.
3. **Who is growing?** The markets up double digits.
4. **What is the constraint?** Safety, visa, housing, cost. The thing content has to solve before anyone books.

The answers set which of the three revenue families leads: **experiences** (visitors), **services and referrals** (movers and retirees), or **sponsorship and B2B** (diaspora, corporate, developers). Manila's answer: services and referrals first, experiences second, sponsorship third. The next cities will answer differently, and until each has its pass these are hypotheses only.

| City | Hypothesis, untested | What the pass has to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| **Manila** (done) | Services and referrals lead. Retirees, returnees, corporate. Experiences for the two-week American visitor. Sponsorship from developers, banks, remittance. | Confirmed in `manila-demand.md`. |
| **Bali** | Experiences lead. Nomads are real here (Canggu has about 15,000 on Nomad List versus Manila's 2,400). Villa and coliving referral, wellness and surf experiences, visa-agent referral. Sponsorship from coliving and coworking brands. | Nomad and long-stay volumes by nationality, Indonesian visa rules and enforcement, villa market saturation, the Canggu overtourism backlash. UNSURE on all of it until researched. |
| **Mexico City** | Experiences and sponsorship lead. About 8,400 nomads and a large US remote-worker base. Food and culture experiences, Spanish schools, but housing referral is politically contested after the 2024 to 2025 anti-gentrification protests. | US and Canadian arrivals, the temporary-resident visa path, the housing politics, safety by colonia. UNSURE until researched. |

**The rule for the repo:** a city page is not built until `research/<city>-demand.md` exists and the slate is written against it. Added to `CLAUDE.md`.
