# 6. Health cover

HMOs named repeatedly: **Maxicare, Medicard, Intellicare**. International: AXA, Allianz, Cigna, April International, IMG.

**The useful distinction for a film, and for Landing:** HMOs are prepaid cashless access to a partner network, cheap and common among expats, but they carry **restricted networks, capped benefit ceilings, referral requirements and age limits**. International cover costs more and travels.

**That age limit is the story.** It is exactly what a retiree needs to know before moving, and it is the kind of thing nobody puts in a brochure. Belongs in `retire.html` and in the SRRV film.

## What this sweep actually concluded

**The partner gap is not suppliers.** Visa agencies, transport, guides, movers, caregivers and brokers are all findable and several are already drafted.

**The gap is audience**, and it does not close by emailing anyone. It closes by publishing films and by sitting at a table in BGC every Wednesday.

Which means the two things that matter most are already written and cost nothing: **`briefs/00-madrid-pre-departure.md`**, and a recurring Wednesday in the landing-week calendar.
