## How food-tour partnerships typically work

**1. Supplier commission (what operators already pay the platforms).** This is the benchmark for any Vaya margin.
- Viator: 20 to 30%, typically 25%, plus a US$29 per-product listing fee since Aug 2025 ([Samba, Apr 2026](https://www.sambahq.com/ota-supplier-guide/ota-commission-rates); [Bokun](https://www.bokun.io/ota-bookings)).
- GetYourGuide: 20 to 30%, set by country and volume ([Samba](https://www.sambahq.com/ota-supplier-guide/ota-commission-rates); [Bokun](https://www.bokun.io/ota-bookings)).
- Klook: about 15 to 25%, negotiated individually ([Bokun Klook guide](https://www.bokun.io/klook-supplier)).
- Airbnb Experiences: flat 20% host service fee ([Airbnb Help](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3164)).
- TripAdvisor: no direct supply; routes through Viator.

**2. Affiliate (zero ops, low margin).** Vaya links out; the platform pays about 8% of booking value with a 30-day cookie ([Viator Partner Program](https://partnerresources.viator.com/), [GetYourGuide Partner](https://partner.getyourguide.com/)). Traffic converts on their brand, not Vaya's.

**3. Reseller or net rate (co-brand, mid margin).** Operator gives Vaya a net rate; Vaya sells at retail on its own page and keeps the spread. Because operators already give 20 to 30% to the platforms, a 15 to 25% Vaya margin is market-normal and cheaper for them. Bokun's Marketplace is the standard plumbing ([Bokun](https://www.bokun.io/ota-bookings)).

**4. White-label "Vaya Experiences" (highest control).** Booking happens under Vaya's brand on white-label checkout tech (Bokun or Regiondo widgets); the operator delivers. Net-rate agreement plus a co-marketing clause. White-label terms are not published and are negotiated per deal ([Bokun](https://www.bokun.io/ota-bookings); [AltexSoft on white-label OTA models](https://www.altexsoft.com/blog/ota-platform/)).

## Read for Vaya

- **Tier 1, true co-brand:** the independents with no platform lock-in. WanderManila, Mstar Tours, Venus, SALO, Renacimiento (editorial), and Old Manila Walks (prestige, founder-led).
- **Volume anchor:** Explore Manila Tours already sells on five platforms. Realistic ask is a net-rate reseller deal plus one exclusive "Vaya edition" route.
- **Chains (A Chef's Tour, Secret Food Tours):** affiliate or reseller only.
- **Watch for exclusivity:** hosts on Airbnb and Traveling Spoon may have platform clauses. Ask before offering white-label.
