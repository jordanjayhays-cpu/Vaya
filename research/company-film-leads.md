# Company film leads: the DTI National Food Fair pool

Date: 2026-09-13. Jordan attended the **2025 DTI Bagong Pilipinas National Food Fair**, 9 to 13 April 2025, Megatrade Halls 1 to 3, SM Megamall. Over 250 MSME food and beverage exhibitors. This is the seed list for the MVP in `research/company-films-mvp.md`.

**Read the accuracy warning before emailing anyone.**

---

## ⚠️ Accuracy warning, read first

**Most of the names below come from coverage of the 2026 fair, not the 2025 one Jordan attended.** It is the same annual event with a heavily overlapping exhibitor pool, but **it is not the same list.**

Writing "I saw your product at the fair in April 2025" to a company that only exhibited in 2026 is an instant credibility loss, and it is exactly the kind of error that kills a cold email.

**The rule: only use the "I saw you there" line for a company Jordan actually remembers, or one confirmed in 2025 coverage.** For everyone else the opening is "I follow the National Food Fair" or nothing at all.

Verified as **2025** exhibitors are marked ✅. Everything else is marked 2026 and needs checking before the line is used.

---

## The three sources that finish this list

1. **The 4K walkthrough of all 250 exhibitors from the 2025 fair** on YouTube ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DWFX7ZBQB_E)). This is the actual 2025 list. Scrub it, pause on booth banners, write down names. An afternoon of work.
2. **DTI Bagong Pilipinas Marketplace** at `dtimarketplace.gov.ph`, the B2B directory launched at the 2025 fair specifically to connect these MSMEs to buyers. It returns 403 to automated access; **Jordan should open it in a browser.**
3. **Go Lokal!** DTI's retail programme: 156 outlets across 24 retail partners. Anything on a Go Lokal shelf has already cleared a retail-readiness screen, which does most of filter 1.

### Bigger names, probably too big to need Vaya
**Auro Chocolate** and **Malagos** are internationally awarded tree-to-bar cacao brands from Davao. Perfect profile on paper, but both already have marketing budgets and press. Worth one email each purely because a yes would be a portfolio piece that sells everything after it.

### Disqualified, and a useful example of why
**Easter Joy Rabbit Ventures.** Founder Alvin Samson is on record saying **demand already exceeds supply.** That fails filter 5: their constraint is capacity, not attention. A film would make their problem worse. **This is the filter doing its job, and it is worth remembering that a company wanting more customers is not the same as a company needing them.**
