## Vaya Home — Move In

**The family is renamed from Property to Home.** Property is a listing site. **Home is what the customer actually wants**, and it is the word that lets the product be aspirational instead of defensive.

**The old Unit Check was the wrong product.** It sold fear: pay us so you do not get scammed. That is a low-status thing to sell, it is small money, and it positions Vaya as an inspector rather than a partner. **The inspection does not disappear. It becomes step three of something bigger.**

### What Move In actually is
**You tell us your budget, your dates and how you live. Three weeks later you have keys, furniture, water, power, internet, and a phone number that works.**

| Step | What Vaya does |
|---|---|
| 1. **The brief** | One call. Budget, dates, work, family, what you cannot live without |
| 2. **The shortlist** | Five real options in the right district, sourced through a licensed broker, with what the brochure crops out |
| 3. **The viewings** | **We go and film every one so you can watch them back.** The lobby at 9pm, the lift at 8am, water pressure, real occupancy, what units actually closed at. This is where the old Unit Check lives now |
| 4. **The lease** | We read it, list what to negotiate, and tell you what will cost you later |
| 5. **Move in** | Utilities, internet installed and chased until it works, cleaning, the basics bought and in place |
| 6. **The first week** | A driver on call, the eSIM and wallet set up, someone on WhatsApp |

| | |
|---|---|
| **Price** | **PHP 45,000** (about US$790). More for a family or a house |
| **Vaya keeps** | **About PHP 35,000.** Broker takes nothing from Vaya; the customer's lease is the broker's business |
| **Who it is for** | The mover, the retiree, the corporate transfer, the returning Fil-Am. Everyone who is staying more than three months |
| **Why it is worth it to them** | A relocation firm charges a corporate several thousand dollars for exactly this. **US$790 is cheap, and it is the difference between arriving and living** |
| **The film** | ***Five days to a home in Makati.*** One real person, five real viewings, the real total at the end |

### Why this beats the Unit Check
- **Three and a half times the margin per engagement**, for maybe twice the work
- **It is a relationship, not a transaction.** Someone you moved into a home needs a visa, a driver, a helper and a tour. The Unit Check customer buys once and vanishes
- **It is aspirational.** People will send it to a friend. Nobody forwards a condo inspection
- **It is filmable.** Five viewings is a film. One inspection is a clip
- **It bundles what Vaya already sells.** Landing, Internet, the eSIM, the lease read, the driver. **Nothing new to build**

### What happened to the Annual Report
**Cut as a headline product.** It is a PHP 1,500 admin chore and it makes Vaya look like a paperwork mill. **It becomes an upsell mentioned to existing Desk customers in January**, not something anyone is sold on. Same treatment as the Unit Check: real, useful, and not the thing on the menu.

## The two decisions that unblock the profitable half

Both are Jordan's and neither needs more research.

**1. The standards page and paid company films.**
`standards.html` publicly says "no business can buy a place in a film." Company films are **29% of the conservative year**. Either amend the page to allow clearly-labelled commissioned films with a permanent recommendation ban, or drop the product. **Detail in `research/company-films-legal.md` section 1.**

**2. Whether Move In needs the RESA answer first.**
Vaya is not brokering: a licensed broker sources and transacts, and Vaya takes a flat fee from the customer for finding, filming, checking and settling them in, with no commission from anyone. That is almost certainly a relocation service rather than real estate practice, but **get it in writing before the first customer.** See `research/real-estate-product.md`.
