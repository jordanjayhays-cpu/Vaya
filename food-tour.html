How it works

Four hours, six stops, one guide.

  1. Saturday afternoon, Makati or BGC
    We tell you where to meet and what to wear. Four hours, mostly walking, one short ride.
  2. Six stops, with the prices said out loud
    Street level and sit down. Your guide tells you what each thing costs a local, which is the part nobody says.
  3. A licensed guide runs it, not us
    A local guide who works these streets. We book it, we film it, and we are there.

    Every stop, with what we paid, so you can go back without us.

Before you ask

The honest part.

  • Six people is the maximum. Past that it stops being a meal with people and becomes a walking tour.
  • We will not run it half price to fill it. If a date does not fill we run it anyway at the same price, or we move it.
  • Tell us about allergies when you book, not on the day. Two of the stops cannot be changed at short notice.
  • It is hot and you will walk about three kilometres. If that is not your afternoon, a driver and a shorter version is the better idea.
