# What happens when you land at NAIA — EP04

**Sells: Vaya Concierge — Landing. Page: `landing.html`.**

## Premise, one line
**"A real arrival, filmed start to finish, including the queue and the taxi touts."**

## The open, first 10 seconds
**Handheld, walking out of the jet bridge, no narration.** The sound of the arrivals hall. Then a single card on screen: the local time.

## The beats
| # | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immigration queue | Real time on screen, counting up. **The wait is the story** |
| 2 | Bags, then the doors | The wall of noise at the exit |
| 3 | **The touts** | Filmed honestly, faces blurred, **no confrontation and no mockery** |
| 4 | The phone problem | No signal, no data, no way to call anyone. **Show the screen** |
| 5 | The money problem | ATM fee on screen. Card declined if it happens |
| 6 | **The alternative, without selling it** | The name card at arrivals. The eSIM handed over. Maya set up in the car |
| 7 | The segue, 12 seconds | "Or someone is waiting with your name and a phone that works." |

## Shot list
- **Clock or timer on screen through the queue section.** The whole tension is duration
- Over-the-shoulder of a phone with no service, then with service
- The ATM screen and the fee
- **The name card, held up, in focus**
- Car interior: the eSIM activating, Maya verifying on a passport
- Arrival at the door, bags in, key in hand
- **Sound: the arrivals hall clean for 60 seconds.** This is a Vibes cut

## The honest beat, do not cut it
**Say on camera that for a two-week trip an Airalo is easier and cheaper.** Telling people not to buy from you is what makes the rest land, and it is already on the page.

## What not to do
- **No filming of immigration officers, counters, or security.** This is a hard rule and it will get your camera taken
- Do not identify a tout. Blur faces
- No "scam" framing of people doing a job

## The end
**"The number in the corner of the screen is the one we called."** Then `vayanews.com/landing`, **EP04**.

## Practical
- **Shoot on Jordan's actual arrival**, day one. It only works once
- Check what may be filmed landside versus airside. **Ask NAIA's PR office in advance**
- Second camera is a phone, which is also the point
