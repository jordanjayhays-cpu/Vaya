# Block by Block, Makati

**The flagship. It sells nothing and it is the most important film on the list.**

## Premise, one line
**"Manila is the second worst city in the world for quality of life. Makati scores 62 where Manila scores 35. Here is what that actually looks like on the ground."**

## Why this one first
Every global index rates "Manila", which is not a place anyone lives. **Correcting them is the single most contrarian, most shareable thing Vaya can publish**, and it establishes the whole premise: we go and look.

## The open, first 10 seconds
Cold. **The Numbeo number on screen over a shot of traffic.** No music yet, no logo, no host. Then cut hard to a quiet Salcedo side street at the same time of day.

> "Same city. Four kilometres apart."

## The beats
| # | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The number everyone quotes | Index rankings, dated, sourced on screen |
| 2 | What "Manila" actually means | Map: the City of Manila, then Metro Manila, then the four square kilometres people actually move to |
| 3 | **Walk Salcedo** | Morning. Coffee, the park, the market on a Saturday |
| 4 | **The rent, said out loud** | A real listing, a real asking price, and **what units in that building actually closed at** |
| 5 | **The honest half** | The flooded corner. The street without a pavement. The 8am lift queue. **Do not skip this** |
| 6 | Legazpi and Poblacion, briefly | So it is a comparison, not an advert |
| 7 | The verdict | Direct to camera, one sentence, no hedging |

## Shot list
- **Two time-of-day passes minimum.** 7am and 9pm on the same street. The gap is the film.
- Handheld walking, waist height, moving with the correspondent
- **Locked-off wide of one intersection for 30 seconds**, used as a cutaway three times
- The lift lobby at 8am. The queue is the shot
- Close: a receipt, a menu, a price tag, a meter
- **Sound: record the street clean for 60 seconds at each location.** Vibes cuts come from this and it costs nothing

## What not to do
- **No drone sunset.** It is the single most brochure thing available and `BRAND.md` bans the register
- No "hidden gem", no "vibrant", no "bustling"
- Do not name a business negatively without the fact-check step

## The end
**No call to action.** Last line is the verdict, then the Vaya card. **This film earns the right to sell in the other three.**

## Practical
- 2 shoot days. Correspondent plus videographer
- Releases for anyone recognisable
- Mall and building interiors need permission. **Ask at the admin office, do not wing it**
