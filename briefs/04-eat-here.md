# Eat Here, Not There — EP05

**Sells: Vaya Tours — Food. Page: `food-tour.html`.**

## Premise, one line
**"The same dish at PHP 180 and PHP 1,200. Here is what you are actually paying for."**

## The open, first 10 seconds
**Two plates, side by side, same dish.** No context. Two prices appear. Hold for two seconds. Then cut to the street.

## The beats
| # | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The two plates | Prices on screen, dated |
| 2 | **The cheap one, made** | The stall. The hands. The wok. The queue of locals |
| 3 | **The expensive one, made** | The restaurant. The plating. The aircon |
| 4 | The honest comparison | **Both are fine.** One is four times the price. Say what the money buys: a chair, aircon, a bathroom |
| 5 | The guide, named | **Disclosure in the same breath: "this place is on our food tour, if you book we make money, and we would send you here anyway"** |
| 6 | Four more stops, fast | This is where the three-second rule earns its keep |
| 7 | The segue, 12 seconds | "The guide in this film runs it on Saturdays." |

## Shot list
- **Overhead of every dish**, consistent framing, price card in frame
- Hands: cooking, paying, eating. **Lots of hands**
- The queue of locals. **That is the proof, not our opinion**
- The guide talking while walking, handheld
- A real receipt, close
- **Sound: the stall clean for 60 seconds.** Sizzle, orders being called

## The disclosure, non-negotiable
**Spoken on camera, in the same breath as the recommendation, every time a business is named.** One of the three lines from `standards.html`. Not in the description. Not at the end.

## What not to do
- **No health claims.** Not "good for you", not "clean", not "safe". FDA rules and a criminal penalty behind them
- Do not name a business negatively without the fact-check step
- Do not film inside without asking the owner. **Ask, release, then shoot**

## The end
**"The guide in this film runs it on Saturdays."** Then `vayanews.com/food-tour`, **EP05**.

## Practical
- **Shoot it on the real tour, with paying guests**, landing week day 6
- Releases from the guide and every guest, collected at the start, not the end
- Owner permission at each of the six stops, **secured the week before**
