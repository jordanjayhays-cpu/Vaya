# The Visa Truth — EP03

**Sells: Vaya Desk — Visa. Page is built: `visa.html`.**

## Premise, one line
**"You get 30 days. You can stay 36 months. The nomad visa everyone talks about does not exist."**

## The open, first 10 seconds
**Jordan's own passport, the arrival stamp, in close.** Finger on the date.

> "Thirty days. Day thirty-one is a fine."

## The beats
| # | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 30 days, and what happens on day 31 | The stamp. The number |
| 2 | **The extension, filmed as it happens** | **Jordan files his own, through the partner agency.** This is the spine of the film |
| 3 | The real cost | Every fee itemised on screen, dated, government fee separated from agency fee |
| 4 | **The 59-day line** | Past it you get an ACR I-Card, which is what unlocks a bank account and full GCash. **Draw the chain on screen** |
| 5 | **The nomad visa that is not real** | EO 86, April 2025, still not on the DFA portal. Show the portal |
| 6 | The annual report nobody mentions | 1 January to 1 March. The fine for missing it |
| 7 | The segue, 12 seconds | "Or we file it and you keep the day." |

## Shot list
- Passport, stamp, fingers, dates. **Macro, lots of it**
- The agency office exterior and the person who files, named and released
- **The courier handover.** Passport going out and coming back
- Screen recording of the DFA e-Visa portal with no nomad visa on it
- A calendar with 1 March circled
- **Jordan's real receipt**, held up

## The number rule
**Every fee on screen gets the date checked.** BI fees change. A wrong number here is the one people will quote back.

## What not to do
- Do not say how to avoid a fee or bend a rule. **Ever**
- Do not imply Vaya files anything. **An accredited agency files. Vaya collects documents**
- No footage inside a BI office without written permission

## The end
**"We file it. You keep the day."** Then `vayanews.com/visa` on screen and in the description, with **EP03**.

- 1 shoot day plus the courier return
- **Shoot this in landing week 1**, because Jordan's own extension is the film
