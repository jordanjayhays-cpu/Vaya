# The Visa Truth — EP02

**Sells: Vaya Desk — Visa. Page is built: `visa.html`.**

## Premise, one line
**"You get 30 days. You can stay 36 months. You can do most of it yourself online, and the nomad visa everyone talks about does not exist."**

> **Rewritten 2026-10-07.** The original premise sold a day saved at the Bureau. The Bureau's eServices portal took that day away. See `research/bi-eservices-flag.md`. **The film now tells people the free option exists, on screen, with the URL.** Anything else is the thing `standards.html` forbids.

## The open, first 10 seconds
**Jordan's own passport, the arrival stamp, in close.** Finger on the date.

> "Thirty days. Day thirty-one is a fine."

## The beats
| # | Beat | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The 30 days, and what happens on day 31 | The stamp. The number |
| 2 | **The portal, on screen, used** | **e-services.immigration.gov.ph. Jordan files his own 9A extension on it, screen recorded, start to payment.** This is now the spine of the film. Show the GCash confirmation |
| 3 | The real cost | Every fee itemised on screen, dated, government fee separated from any agency fee |
| 4 | **What the portal will not do** | **The 59-day line.** Past it you need an ACR I-Card and they take your fingerprints, so you appear in person. Hearings, and a record that does not match. **Draw the chain on screen** |
| 5 | **The nomad visa that is not real** | EO 86, April 2025, still not on the DFA portal. Show the portal |
| 6 | The annual report nobody mentions | 1 January to 1 March. The fine for missing it. **And the open contradiction: the portal takes it, Bureau notices have said appear in person. Say on camera that we do not know yet** |
| 7 | The segue, 12 seconds | "Most of you should just use the portal. Ask us your dates anyway, because that is the part nobody tells you." |

## Shot list
- Passport, stamp, fingers, dates. **Macro, lots of it**
- **Screen recording of e-services.immigration.gov.ph, full, unhurried.** The form, the fee, the payment, the approval email. This is the most useful thirty seconds in the film
- The agency office exterior and the person who files, named and released. **Only for the part the portal cannot take**
- **The ACR I-Card biometrics queue**, or its door if filming inside is not permitted
- Screen recording of the DFA e-Visa portal with no nomad visa on it
- A calendar with 1 March circled
- **Jordan's real receipt**, held up

## The number rule
**Every fee on screen gets the date checked.** BI fees change. A wrong number here is the one people will quote back.

## What not to do
- **Do not leave the portal out, do not bury it, do not mention it once and move on.** It gets its own beat and its own URL on screen. A film that hides the free option to sell the paid one is exactly what `standards.html` forbids
- **Do not claim the annual report question is settled.** Say on camera that we do not know, and that the answer will be published on `vayanews.com/visa`
- Do not say how to avoid a fee or bend a rule. **Ever**
- Do not imply Vaya files anything. **An accredited agency files. Vaya collects documents**
- No footage inside a BI office without written permission

## The end
**"The filing is twenty minutes and it is free. Missing the date is a fine and a lawyer. We are only useful for the second one."**

Then `vayanews.com/visa` on screen and in the description, with **EP02**.
