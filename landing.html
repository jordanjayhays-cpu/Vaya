Before you ask

The honest part.

  • Airalo and Holafly are easier, and for two weeks they are the right answer. They are data only, with no phone number, so nothing here that needs an SMS code will work. A month or more and you need a +63 number.
  • A tourist SIM stops working after about 30 days unless you show an approved visa extension. A dead SIM locks your wallet, because every login here verifies by text. Worth knowing on day one, not day 31.
  • GCash needs a Philippine government ID. Maya verifies on a passport, which is why we set up Maya.
  • If someone is already meeting you, do not pay us. Say so and we will send you the eSIM instructions for free.
